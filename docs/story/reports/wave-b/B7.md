## What plays, beat by beat

1. **arrive.** In at the door, the door swinging shut behind me. "They're coming here." Dot comes up from under the counter with the Tommy gun and lays it on its side before me, then two drums beside it (her own clip, `b7_dotGun`; the gun and the drums ride its paths, so the object comes first and her arms follow it). E takes it: I lean over the counter a little if I stand back, the hands close on the gun where it lies, lift it off the counter and down out of the frame, and it comes up to the shoulder with the bolt racked. The switch by the door (E) puts the room's lamps out: pendants, their shades and their pools go dark, and the men outside shoot worse at a dark room (aim x 0.7).
2. **cars.** The music comes in. Dot ducks. Two sedans come up the avenue, headlamps first, swing hard across the far side of the road with a skid and stop nose-in toward the diner; their lamps come through the windows as window-shaped bars across the room (the train's light bars, driven by where each beam meets the front wall), and as volumetric beams in the rain. Doors open one at a time (hinged leaves, a dark hole behind), six men step out behind them; their first rounds go into the glass, one pane at a time (the first round stars a pane, the second brings it down), a pendant over the booths is shot out and swings, the bottles on the back bar burst when hit. The cars are the block's car model on my own rails: they sit on the road, dip on the brakes, roll on the swing, are shot like any car (glass, smoke, fire, a blast) and are cover for the men (the Hoods' grid and cover points follow each car and each open door). The beat ends when two of the six are left.
3. **doorways.** Three more from across the street, out of the dark under the el's far side, through the columns and into the road; then one I hear before I see: out of the side alley, down the pavement, the bell of the door, in. Three men may fire at once from here on (director level 5).
4. **el.** A man at the el's west rail, above the lead car, fires down (his first burst chews the iron and the street: the tell). Hit twice, he crumples, tips over the rail and drops onto the car's roof (a thud, the car rocks, its glass goes).
5. **driveby.** A car comes down the near side of the road and does not stop: a Tommy gun from its back window along the diner's front (its first rounds into the facade, then at me). Kill the driver or wreck the engine and it goes off its line into what it swerves least for ahead of it (the lamp post on its own side before an el column across the road) and burns (the post goes dark, the riders die in it). Left alone, it turns at the end of the block and comes back up the far side once, firing again, and is gone.
6. **cardinal.** Whoever is still up after a while pulls back down the avenue. The music goes (4 s), a quiet (7 s of rain). A long black saloon rolls up under the lamp north of the diner, handbrake; two heavies with Tommy guns out of the front doors; then him, out of the back: the Cardinal (cast kind `cardinal`): wide, slow, a homburg, a long coat with a fur shawl collar, a cane in his left hand, a pistol in his right, a cigar with its ember. He walks at the diner behind his men and stops to fire now and then. He takes a great deal of killing and shows it: the hat stays on; after 5 wounds his walk slows, after 10 a knee goes (down on it 1.8 s, then up and on, slower); after 15 he falls on his back.
7. **end.** Whoever is left breaks and runs. The gun lowers by itself and will not fire. His hat comes off in the fall, rolls on its brim in an arc, turns and settles in the water; the cigar lies beside it, its ember still breathing (the one accent), the rain hissing on it. I walk out to it (a hint after 9 s: `WASD  out to him`); behind me Dot's shape stands in the broken window. Near it the view goes down on one knee by his head, the hat and the ember low in the frame, the wet street and the lamp's cone beyond; the el train goes over; `end` is cued; six seconds; THE END card (`hud.showEnd({ kind: 'end' })`, its click goes to the title). No credits.

Dying: 0.9 s of falling, then black and back at the start of the beat I was in (`scenes.go(..., { fade: 'black', card: false })`, about 3 s in all), my ammunition and the lights as they were when the beat began; the room keeps its broken glass, bottles and dead men, the beat's own cars are sent round again.

Ammunition: the gun comes with a full drum and two more on the counter (the HUD shows the drums I carry as rings over the count); at the counter, E rakes them in; when I have none Dot slides two more up the counter (E again). The trigger on an empty gun clicks, and the HUD says where the drums are. Dot cannot be hurt.

## Open (most felt first)

1. **Too easy and short for the driver, first-timer length unmeasured.** The driver never dies more than once and takes 24-131 damage; the scene runs 115-148 game seconds for it. The cars beat can be over in 11 s when the men are shot as they step out (their first rounds go into the glass, not at me). Levers, all in `src/scenes/09_shootout.js` / `site.js`: the men's `aim` (cars 1.25, doorways 1.15, intruder 1.35, heavies 1.0, x 0.7 in the dark), the beat ends (`alive('cars') <= 2`, `alive('doorways') <= 1`, time-outs 50 / 45 / 35 s), a floor on the cars beat's length, men who stay behind their doors longer.
2. **The el gunman is hard to see.** He stands behind the rail's lattice 10.7 m up; from the diner he is a small shape 20 m off, from under the el the deck's edge hides all but his head. His muzzle flash reads (the tell); his fall onto the car's roof reads only from the street.
3. **The cars' wheels neither turn nor steer**, which the brief asks for: the block's car is one merged mesh with symmetric hubcaps; it needs the wheels split out in `Cars.js` (a shared file) or in my rails.
4. **Car doors do not read at night**: the leaf and the hole behind it are black on a black car; in the cars strip the men appear beside the cars rather than coming out of them.
5. **Taking the drums is a scoop**: E at the counter rakes the stack off its edge out of the frame (no hand on them); Dot's two new drums slide up the counter on their own (her arm does not push them).
6. **The survivors' retreat is crude**: when they break (before the quiet, and when he falls) they run straight down the avenue with no path-finding and are removed at 30 m or after 8 s, wherever they are (one could vanish in view, or run into a car).
7. **Dot at the window is a pop**: she is placed there when I am out and not looking toward it; she does not walk there.
8. **Not verified in a capture**: the drive-by's second pass (left alone it turns at the end of the block and comes back up the far side), the el train in the last seconds (cued over the frame, passing overhead out of it), the intruder being "heard on the step" (generic footsteps and the door's bell, no sound of its own).
9. **The Cardinal's knee and fall**: his knee is his own clip (`cardinal_knee`), his fall is the Hood rig's `deathBack`; the ground contact in the fall was only checked by eye in one strip. The hat's roll is a simple arc on its brim.
10. **The slice play's last check fails on this branch by design**: `tools/plays/slice.json` expects "To be continued" after the fight, but `ctx.next()` from the fight now finds scene 9 (the only later scene on this branch). With scenes 3-8 merged it goes to `body`.
11. **Frames per second**: the cars beat runs at about 0.75 of `shot=perf` (0.60-1.10 over four alternated runs on a GPU other builders kept busy). It is heavier than the perf scene in draw calls (823 vs 726-778): the rails' door leaves and holes, the cones, the shards and the back-bar bottles are separate meshes. Not yet cut.
12. `G.b7` (the scene's rig) and `G.director.level` are set on the game at run time (not a shared-file change): teardown clears them when the scene exits.
