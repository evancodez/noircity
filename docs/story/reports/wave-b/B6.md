## What plays, beat by beat

`?start=warehouse` plays from my car to the pier past the bay by real input
(`tools/plays/warehouse.json`), never seen, in about 3 min 40 s of game time.

1. **arrive**: by my car in the rain, the gate behind me, PIER 9 painted on the
   shed's boards, a freighter's black hull with its one riding light along the
   east side, the caged lamp over the side door twenty metres off. **E** opens it.
2. **floor**: inside the door, in the dark. The first dock hand (lantern) is at
   the truck's tail; as I come in he walks west through the door's pool and
   turns up aisle A. While he is near and I am standing, the one prompt:
   **C crouch**. The eye drops 0.62 m in a fifth of a second, the hands ride
   lower, the pace falls to 2.05 m/s (walk 3.1, run 5.4; running is heard at
   8 m, walking at 3 m, a crouch not at all).
3. **lamps**: up aisle A behind him into a dark pocket at the cross aisle. When
   he has gone on up his aisle: a bottle from a low crate (**E**), the knife
   switch on the corner post (**E**; the left hand pulls it when the right
   holds a bottle): aisle B's three lamps flicker and die. Aisle B's own man
   walks over (about 16 s from his round), stands 8 s at the panel with his
   back to the floor, and puts it back. The chain hoist is here too: **E** at
   its cleat drops the load at the far end of aisle C with a crash, and the
   two nearest men go to look.
4. **aisle** (a checkpoint): from the pocket by the second stack, up aisle A
   behind the first man's back to the pocket in the far stacks.
5. **stairs**: two Hoods at the foot of the stairs (one holding his post with a
   cigarette, one walking the strip with a torch). From the strip's end,
   **hold LMB**: the inked arc and where it lands; let go: the bottle lobs
   along the strip and breaks between them, both turn and go to look. While
   they look I slip along the west wall in behind the stores under the
   mezzanine (a row of crates two high: a dark way along the back wall to the
   stairs' foot). When the Hood at the stairs is back at his post with his back
   to them and the torch has set off east, out to the foot and up the stairs.
6. **listen**: crouched on the catwalk under the office window. Behind the
   blinds, black shapes on the lit back wall: the Cardinal (lane B7's
   `cardinal` when it merges; until then a wide Hood stand-in, the cigarette's
   ember the one accent) and two men. Three slow subtitles: "The diner.
   Tonight." / "The waitress saw Mack talk." / "No one leaves." He comes to the
   window and looks down for 3.2 s: standing, or moving faster than a creep
   within 5.5 m, is the alarm.
7. **out**: the truck's engine; it backs against the side door I came by, and
   the Hood with the torch walks down to meet it. Along the catwalk, down the
   east stairs behind the bay man's back, along the bay's east wall, **E** on
   the bay's side door, out on the apron in the rain: `ctx.next()` three metres
   past the door.

**The men.** Four dock hands (`dockhand`: flat cap, short jacket, a cargo hook
at the belt, a lantern or a torch) and two Hoods. Calm: he walks his round,
stops, looks where the round says, moves on. **Curious**: he stops, a low "hm",
his head and then his light come round to what he heard or glimpsed; it takes
2.5 s to be sure in the dark, 1 s in a pool (faster near); out of his sight it
drains and he goes on the way he was going. **Search**: he walks to where I was,
looks about, gives up (a shrug). **Alarm**: a whistle, they all come; 1.7 s
later the cosh from behind, black, and back at the last checkpoint (the door,
the pocket at the cross aisle by the hoist, the second stack, the stairs, the
window, the catwalk), the room as it was.

**Their eyes are their lights.** A man sees me if his beam is on me, or if I
stand in a lamp's light anywhere in front of him (a torch's own light on me
is his beam, not a lamp), or within 3 m in the dark in front of him; every
sighting needs a clear line (a ray, every 0.1 s). A step within its radius
turns him curious; a crash or a bottle sends the nearest two to look. A man
found lying: whoever finds him goes to him with the nearest one and they
search. **From behind**: **E** on a man who has not noticed me (within 1.7 m,
more than 110 degrees off where he faces and looks): one short blow, he folds.
**The revolver** stays in the coat: LMB with nothing in hand, the hand goes to
the coat and comes away.
