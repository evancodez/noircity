## What plays, beat by beat

**`seen`** (room 304's street window; the backlot's stand-in corner of the
room until lane B2's office merges, see Open). The chapter card, then the
room comes up out of the black: the blind in front of me, slivers of the
lamp across the street through its slats. **E look out**: my right hand
comes up and two fingers press the slat at my eye down (object first: the
fingertips are put on the slat's edge each frame while it bends), the slat
over it lifts, and the eye leans to the slit on the line to him while the
lens closes from 68 to 44 degrees. Across the street, in his lamp's cone,
under his umbrella, a man looking up at this window. Held a second and a
half; he starts (a notice clip), the umbrella tips out of his hand, drops
and rolls on its rim in the wind until it lies on its side in the wet
(never through the street, never in the air), and he runs for the side
alley under the window. The chase's music slot is cued at the drop. The fingers let the
slat go and it springs shut. **E the fire escape**: the head turns to the
side window (0.4 s) and the body follows it across the room and out onto
the iron (0.82 s), a cutscene track, no cut.

**`escape`**. Third floor, iron and rain. The escape mode walks a rail of
the fire escape's landings and flights under W (fast: 3.3 m/s on the level,
2.9 on the stairs, two flights and the landings in about six seconds; S
holds), each tread clangs and each landing rings, a hand on the flight's
handrail; the mouse looks where it likes within 80 degrees of the way and
the turns at the landings sweep the view round on their own. At L1 the drop ladder slides
down under my weight (it rides with me and stops with a clank and a
shake), and the eye comes round to face up the alley as I land. Below,
across the street and into the alley's mouth: him, paced by my progress
down the iron so he is fifteen metres up the alley when I land. A "W down"
prompt appears only if I stand still on the top landing for 2.5 s.

**`run`**. The run mode: W runs at 7.5 m/s (no key to hold for it), the
mouse steers as in walking, the lens opens 5.5 degrees at speed, the bob is
harder, both fists pump in the bottom corners, my breath with the stride,
my feet in the water. Five stretches, each ending in a turn:
- *the straight* (the side alley, 37 m): bins and puddles, two wall lamps,
  the blue HOTEL neon (accent) at the end of the sight line, him fifteen
  metres ahead, pale raincoat, one hand on his bowler.
- *the fences* (the back lane): he skids on the wet at the corner (his feet
  go out, a spray, the slap), then vaults both board fences (a hand on the
  top, the legs over, root motion). **SPACE vault** is taught at the first
  fence by one prompt and never again. Within 2.2 m moving at it (a press is
  remembered 0.3 s, held counts): both palms plant on the top rail either
  side of where the eye crosses it, the eye arcs over and lands with a dip,
  0.42-0.5 s, no speed lost. Without SPACE: a scramble, 1.1 s.
- *the washing* (the yard): four lines of wet sheets, back-lit by a low lamp
  over a door at the yard's far end, thrown down the yard through them. He
  goes in among them; they swing aside and close behind him and me (a
  damped pendulum per sheet, the wind's flutter). A man just past a line is
  his own shadow on the lit sheet.
- *the dog and the crates* (a 2.4 m passage): a bare lamp in a pen behind a
  gate; as I come level the dog hits the gate (it jumps on its hinges, the
  bark, my view flinches). He yanks the crate stacks down behind him (two
  stacks, three crates each, toppling about their bottom edges into the way,
  a gap by the far wall always left). A / D steps round them, SPACE hops
  over (0.42 s); running into one is a stumble (a second at a jog, the hands
  catch, never a stop) and the crate skids on ahead of me off my line.
- *the dead end*: a wall. He is gone; a board in the west fence is still
  swinging. Through it, the street.

**`car`**. Through the board: his sedan at the kerb; he is in it (a get-in
clip, the door's thunk), its lamps come on (the head lamps' cones in the
rain, the tail lamps red: crew), the engine, and it pulls out and away down
the wet street under the lamps on its own rail (to 15 m/s). The eye is
drawn to it, softly (the mouse still turns me; the draw only leans the
look), for 5.4 s, then drifts back to my coupe at the kerb. **E drive**: a
step to the door, my hand under the handle, the click, and `ctx.next()`
(the tail, or the to-be-continued card while scene 7 is not merged).

**The rules under it** (`src/scenes/watcher/chase.js`). He runs the route
(a polyline down the middle of the way) at a speed that keeps him 9 to 22
m ahead: a burst to 9.6 m/s if I close to 5, a tired 4.5 when I fall past
22. More than 30 m behind, or six seconds with no clear line to his head or
chest (his hat over a fence is him; close behind him round a corner is not
lost): "Lost him." (one line), black 0.35 s, back at the start of that
stretch with him fifteen metres ahead, what he knocked down since put back,
the black lifted in 0.45 s. The first seconds of a stretch (the run's cold
start, the fade up after a loss) are a grace: he does not pull away from a
man still finding his feet and cannot be lost (2 to 2.5 s). A player who
runs and presses SPACE at fences makes it; one who stops to look around
loses him in about four seconds.

## What I measured

- **The plays, by real input** (`tools/play.mjs`, trusted keys and mouse
  moves). `tools/plays/watcher.json` from `?start=watcher`: the window, E,
  the slats, E, down the iron with W, the chase with W and the mouse and
  SPACE held down the lane, round the crates by the gaps he leaves, through
  the board, the car, E drive: 14 / 14, twice in a row (the last runs: 47.3
  and 51.4 s of scene time, never lost). `tools/plays/watcher_lost.json`
  from `?start=watcher.run`: stand and look round, "Lost him." after about 4
  s (2 of grace, then 15 to 30 m), black, back at the ladder's foot, him
  15-24 m up the alley jogging, then the retry run to the yard with no
  second loss: 10 / 10.
- **What made the play flaky, and the fix.** A state recorder on a failing
  run (load average 40-56, eight builders): at 7.5 m/s one poll of the
  walker stepped past the corner point at the end of the straight, the
  walker released W and turned round for it (1.5 s standing at a wall), and
  the gap went 22 to 30: lost. A human never does that; the walker now
  counts a point already behind it along its leg (and within 2.5 m) as
  reached, in the run mode only (a `[B4]` change in `tools/play.mjs`, see
  Shared files), and the play has fewer waypoints.
- **Timings in play** (game time): the slit 0.6 s to open (hand 0.3, slat
  0.3), held 1.5 s, the notice to the drop 0.35 s, he is out of the slit in
  about a second; out of the window 0.82 s; the escape 6-7 s; my vault
  0.42-0.5 s over the rail (the palms on it 0.55 m out), the scramble 1.1
  s, a hop 0.42 s, a stumble 0.9 s at a jog; his vault clip 0.8 s (hand on
  the rail 0.14, over 0.34, down 0.66); the car's lamps 0.5 s after he is
  in, it moves 0.9 s later; E drive to `ctx.next()` 0.85 s.
- **Frames per second**, 1440 x 900, against `shot=perf` at the same moment
  (load average 30-56; perf itself swung 25-36): the fire escape (the
  heaviest: 810 draw calls, 0.77 M triangles) 59.5 and 40 fps against perf
  35.5 and 26 run between them (1.5-1.7 x perf); the washing and the
  straight 39 fps against perf 25-27 (1.5 x); the car pulling away 43; the
  slit 60. Every moment of the scene is lighter than perf.
- **The gate.** `npm run build` clean. `python3 tools/golden.py check`: 32

  capture of the presets above reported an empty errlog); `audiotest=1`

## Open (most felt first)

1. **The first beat in lane B2's office has never run.** B2 is not merged
   here, so `seen` plays in the backlot's stand-in corner of room 304 (its
   own blind, a minimal dark room: that is what every frame of `seen` in
   this report shows). The office path (`seenInOffice` in the scene) is
   written against B2's anchors (`office.window.street`,
   `office.street.watcher`, `office.window.escape`) and, as B2's branch has
   it today, parts B2's own blind (`office.part('room').blind`: the slat
   under my eye gets `sag`, the one over it `lift`) and closes the lens to
   44 degrees - but it does not put my hand on the slat (the `partSlats`
   clip reads my stand-in blind's `fingerAt`), his run off in the office's
   street is a straight slide at 6.5 m/s (no route, no lamp), and the move
   from B2's window to my fire escape is a cut on black (0.25 + 0.35 s)
   between two stages that each build the Harbor Building's fire escape.
   At integration: give B2's blind a `fingerAt` / `gapAt` like
   `src/scenes/watcher/blind.js` and run the stand-in path's script on it
   (it is the same beat), and make the two fire escapes agree.
2. **Length.** The scripted play (a perfect route) is 47-51 s of scene
   time; I estimate a first-timer at 55-70 s; the brief asks 60-90. The
   chase is about 160 m, ladder to board, at 7.5 m/s (23-26 s of it); lengthening it means another
   stretch, not a slower run.
3. **His shadow on the washing** shows clearly only when he is a line or
   two ahead of me (review frame 05: he is 5 m past the sheet). At the
   chase's usual 9-22 m the lines between us are in the way and his
   shadow, thrown through every sheet by the low flood over the far door,
   lands small and low on the nearest one, and I could not find it in the
   strips. The flood now always casts its shadows while I am in the yard
   (a `keen` lamp in the pool's deal); what is missing is staging: slow him
   in the yard, or the sheets nearer the flood, so the brief's moment
   happens every time.
4. **The dead end's way out reads only as a slit of street light** where
   the loose board hangs askew (frame 09, at the far end of the fence);
   his wet prints to it are built (`parts/chase.js`) but I could not find
   them in any frame: the ground's puddle pattern swallows them. No one has been stuck in the plays (they know where it is); a
   first-timer may take a few seconds. There is no fail there (he is in
   his car by then), so it costs time, not a retry.
5. **The hand at the slit** is large and flat at the 44-degree lens (the
   back of the hand fills the lower right): the fingertips sit on the slat
   and it reads, but a pose with the hand lower and only two fingers in the
   gap would be better. The door handle at the end is the opposite: a small
   hand on a black car side for 0.85 s before the cut.
6. **Wall lamps in the narrow passage** still paint a soft lit oval on
   their own wall right under them (narrower now and aimed straight down,
   so the opposite wall is black and the old grey eggs at eye height are
   gone). It is the light's true footprint, but the ink leaves it a smooth
   gradient as you pass under.
7. **His raincoat is pale**, not a black mass: chosen so he reads at 25 m in
   black alleys (he does, at every stretch, and goes black against a lamp
   behind him). The inside of his coat tails shows dark as they flare,
   which in the side-on strip can look like a satchel at his hip.
8. **The walls' white brush scratches** (the house ink on wet brick) are
   busy in the dark alleys, where a wall is most of the frame.
9. The stand-in room 304 has no room tone of its own (the street's rain);
   B2's office has one.
10. Captures during the chase: on this machine a capture costs 2-3 s of
    standing still, which loses him; the plays take none between the ladder
    and the board, and the review frames are shot-mode presets
    (`shot=chase&st=<0..4>` with `at=`, `hz=`, `fov=`, `w=0`, `jump=1`: see
    `src/debug/shots/B4_watcher.js`).
