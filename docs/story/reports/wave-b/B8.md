## What plays, beat by beat

1. **The way in** (scene 2's new first beat, `start`; `scenes/fight/start.js`,
   controller mode `player/modes/brawlIn.js`). His line said, the diner hands
   the room to scene 2 with no cut. He stands off his stool (it goes over;
   the room starts to clear, the needle may jump), comes down the counter
   behind my right shoulder and lays his left hand on it: his palm solved onto
   my shoulder's place every step (the cast's IK, `cast/diner/ik.js`, through
   `handsOn` of `cast/diner/dot.js`), the forearm in at the right of the frame while my look lags
   behind him. He pulls me round on the stool (the view swings to him, the
   stool squeaks). I come up and back a step; my left hand takes the stool I
   sat on by its seat (the hand solved onto the moving seat's rim every step,
   `clips/fight.js` `REACH`) and sends it skidding into the counter's foot,
   where it goes over and lies, in every later beat and retry too
   (`layStool`). Fists up from below the frame; he gives ground, squares up.
   About four seconds (the fight play holds it to 3.2 .. 5.4 s), the look free within
   0.3 rad the whole way. Then the lesson's first card. A retry never plays
   it again. `?scene=fight&beat=start&t=<s>` scrubs it.
2. **The lesson**, nine cards (FIGHT.md §7): left jab, right jab, the guard
   up (he answers my first punch into it, slowed), TAP TO JAB / HOLD TO LOAD,
   the hook from each side, **new: his throw** (he takes a plate, backs off,
   throws, the chevron shows the side), the block, the grab. 20 to 29 s for
   the autoplayer, about 30 s by real input.
3. **The open fight.** Measured, dirty, wild, out on his feet, the finish
   out through the nearer window, the walk out, the search, the matchbook
   (unchanged from Wave A after the knock-out).

How it feels now, honestly: a tap is a jab at once; a hold is something I
can see and hear load (the fist snaps back further at each of three ticks
that stay by it, a knock that rises, the view leaning back, a flash of
strokes at full) and it lands big: a level-3 punch stops the world six
steps, puts a 245 px burst on him and drives him back a metre and a half.
Punching into his guard now costs: he answers straight back three times in
four with a short cocked straight (a 0.2 s tell); a quick hop gets me out
of a jab's answer, a blocked hard punch is paid for. He uses the room about
one action in four while something is within his reach and I am not on
top of him (3 to 7 things a fight): plates and mugs come at me on a real
arc aimed where I stood at the tell's start, with the chevron telling me
where to step; stools come round wide. A chair or a stool in my hands is
seen held up before me in both hands and hits for 35 through his guard.
The fight holds together for a reader and falls apart for a masher, which
is what the user asked.

## Open

Most felt first.

1. **His thrown plate is hard to see in flight.** It is small and dark
   against the dark room at four or five metres (strip `s4`): the chevron
   and his big wind-up carry the read, not the plate. A white rim, a
   trail or a larger plate would fix it.
2. **A player who keeps going down in one manner can be stuck there.**
   Each down in a manner gives him back more (8 %, then 16 %, 24 %... of
   his hit points, never past the manner's top), which is what makes the
   masher lose; but it catches a struggling player too. By real input under a fully busy
   GPU, one fight of seven took 235.7 s with 6 downs, 163.5 s of it in his
   measured manner, and one 151 s; the other five were 79 to 110 s. The
   seeded reader never went down more than twice. A softer rule for the
   fourth down and on (the give-back held at 24 %, or his breath shorter)
   is the next thing to try, measured against the masher's seeds.
3. **Of all his actions, fewer than one in four are with a thing.** The rule
   is one of his free choices in four while something is within 3.2 m and
   I am not on top of him; measured, 3 to 7 things against 25 to 40 attacks
   a fight (about one in six), because answers, combos and feints are not
   free choices and the things run out.
4. **His hand on my shoulder** fills the lower right of the frame for about
   a second (`01_shoulder`); it reads as a hand, but a smaller forearm or
   a lower grip would crowd the view less.
5. **A jab no longer stops his blows** (version 3 let a jab caught at his
   tell cancel his straight). It was what made mashing win; now only a
   loaded punch or a step stops him. A player who learnt version 3 will
   feel it.
6. **The lesson teaches his answer once**, slowed, at the guard card; after
   that it is learnt by being hit.
7. **A masher can never finish him**: the finish wants a loaded punch or a
   thing. By design (no masher reached his last manner in ten seeds), but
   the card at the finish says so only once.
8. **The `fight` music slot is not cued** (the jukebox carries the scene;
   see Music).
9. The two-handed stool and chair poses and the `blockBig` / `hop*Big`
   poses were checked in the `b8duel` and `b8things` frames only, not in a
   long play by eye.
10. The golden `fight_round1` needs the art director's accept (see the gate).
11. The machine's disk filled up late in this lane; nothing of mine was lost
    but two review frames, which were captured again.

## How to rerun

- Seeds: `?shot=r2play&beat=lesson&seed=<n>&style=<player|mash|nododge|fists|things>&t=220`,
  then read `__noir.game.brawl.stats` (`won`, `time`, `downs`, `lesson`,
  `lessonSteps`, `answers`, `hisItems`, `hurtBy`, `dmgBy`, `levels`).
- A moment of the fight: `?shot=b8duel&do=wind|answer|full|throw|stoolSwing|mything&...&t=<s>`
  (options in `src/debug/shots/B8_fight.js`); the room from outside the eye:
  `?shot=b8spy&sc=fight&beat=start&spy=x,y,z,yaw,pitch&t=<s>`.
- Things in hands: `?shot=b8things` then `window.__b8things`.
- Plays: `node tools/play.mjs tools/plays/fight.json` (`?start=fight`), `slice.json`,
  `autoplay.json`, `continue.json`.
