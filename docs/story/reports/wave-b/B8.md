## What plays, beat by beat

1. **The way in** (scene 2's new first beat, `start`; `scenes/fight/start.js`,
   controller mode `player/modes/brawlIn.js`). His line said, the diner hands
   the room to scene 2 with no cut. He stands off his stool (it goes over;
   the room starts to clear, the needle may jump), comes down the counter
   behind my right shoulder and lays his left hand on it: his palm solved onto
   my shoulder's place every step (the cast's IK, `cast/diner/ik.js` through
   `handsOn`), the forearm in at the right of the frame while my look lags
   behind him. He pulls me round on the stool (the view swings to him, the
   stool squeaks). I come up and back a step; my left hand takes the stool I
   sat on by its seat (the hand solved onto the moving seat's rim every step,
   `clips/fight.js` `REACH`) and sends it skidding into the counter's foot,
   where it goes over and lies, in every later beat and retry too
   (`layStool`). Fists up from below the frame; he gives ground, squares up.
   About four seconds (3.2 to 5.4 by real input), the look free within
   0.3 rad the whole way. Then the lesson's first card. A retry never plays
   it again. `?scene=fight&beat=start&t=<s>` scrubs it.
2. **The lesson**, nine cards (FIGHT.md §7): left jab, right jab, the guard
   up (he answers my first punch into it, slowed), TAP TO JAB / HOLD TO LOAD,
   the hook from each side, **new: his throw** (he takes a plate, backs off,
   throws, the chevron shows the side), the block, the grab. 20 to 29 s for
   the autoplayer, about 30 s by real input.
3. **The open fight.** Measured, dirty, wild, out on his feet, the finish
   out through the nearer window, the walk out, the search, the matchbook
   (unchanged from Wave A after the knock-out).

How it feels now, honestly: a tap is a jab at once; a hold is something I
can see and hear load (the fist snaps back further at each of three ticks
that stay by it, a knock that rises, the view leaning back, a flash of
strokes at full) and it lands big: a level-3 punch stops the world six
steps, puts a 245 px burst on him and drives him back a metre and a half.
Punching into his guard now costs: he answers straight back three times in
four with a short cocked straight; a quick hop gets me out of a jab's
answer, a blocked hard punch is paid for. He uses the room a third of the
time he has a free choice and something is near: plates and mugs come at
me on a real arc with the chevron telling me where to step, stools come
round wide. A chair or a stool in my hands is seen held up before me and
hits for 35 through his guard. The fight holds together for a reader and
falls apart for a masher, which is what the user asked.

The fight keeps Wave A's choice: the diner's jukebox carries scene 2
(`jukebox_b`, or silence when his rise knocks the needle off, half the

with no file in it stops the jukebox. For the user's cut, a first-timer's
scene 2 from his line to the chapter card: the way in 4 s, the lesson 25 to
35 s, the fight 40 to 80 s after it (the autoplay's whole fight 66 to 99 s,
the real-input driver's 118 to 124 s), then the walk out and the matchbook
about 30 s: two to two and a half minutes in all.

## How to rerun

- Seeds: `?shot=r2play&beat=lesson&seed=<n>&style=<player|mash|nododge|fists|things>&t=220`,
  then read `__noir.game.brawl.stats` (`won`, `time`, `downs`, `lesson`,
  `lessonSteps`, `answers`, `hisItems`, `hurtBy`, `dmgBy`, `levels`).
- A moment of the fight: `?shot=b8duel&do=wind|answer|full|throw|stoolSwing|mything&...&t=<s>`
  (options in `src/debug/shots/B8_fight.js`); the room from outside the eye:
  `?shot=b8spy&sc=fight&beat=start&spy=x,y,z,yaw,pitch&t=<s>`.
- Things in hands: `?shot=b8things` then `window.__b8things`.
- Plays: `node tools/play.mjs tools/plays/fight.json` (`?start=fight`), `slice.json`,
  `autoplay.json`, `continue.json`.
