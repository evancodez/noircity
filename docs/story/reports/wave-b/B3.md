## What plays

**The .38.** `weapons.equip('revolver')` puts it in hand; the front the
other lanes build on is unchanged (six rounds, LMB, R). In order, as a
player feels it:

- **The draw** (0.35 s). The right hand comes up out of the coat with the
  gun and settles into the aim, fast at first then easing in. You hear
  cloth and leather. `holster()` reverses it (0.35 s) and then empties the
  hands. `equip()` always draws.
- **At rest.** One-handed, arm out, the gun low in the right third of the
  frame, turned into it so you see its left side: the fluted cylinder, the
  frame, the barrel pointing to the centre, a slight cant. It follows the
  look with a spring lag, and has a walk bob and a breath. The hammer is
  cocked. Bare right hand, coat sleeve, white shirt cuff.
- **Fire, on the press, this step.** The ray, the flash sprite, the flash
  light and the sound all happen in the step that reads the press, and the
  hands are re-laid in that same step, so the recoil is in the next frame.
  The hammer falls and the index pulls. The muzzle climbs hard about the
  wrist (0.3 rad peak at 42 ms) with a shove back and a little roll to the
  right; it is back in about a quarter of a second (strip `shot`). Then the
  thumb flicks toward the spur and the hammer comes back (0.13 to 0.24 s)
  with two clicks, and the cylinder turns the next chamber up. The view
  kicks 1.1 degrees and returns; the aim is never moved for you. The
  report is deeper and longer than the Hoods' pistol (offline render: peak
  1.28, rms 0.16, 0.7 s, against the pistol's 0.99 / 0.076 / 0.45), with a
  slap and the street's tail on the reverb send, and it ducks the rain.
  The gap between shots is 0.3 s. A press in the last 0.15 s of the gap is
  kept and fires as soon as the gap ends. Holding the button does not
  repeat. A press on an empty chamber clicks, and if the cylinder is empty
  the reload starts.
- **Hits** use the Tommy gun's bullet path: `rays.raycastHoods`, then
  `rays.raycast` / `react.bullet`, so impacts, glass and breakables all
  react. A body hit does 55 and always staggers (`h.stagger()` plus the
  brain's `onHit(dir, true)`). Two body hits kill a Hood (100); a head hit
  (999) kills. A heavy (170) takes four body hits. Blood comes from
  `bloodFx.hit`. Any Hood the round passes near ducks: the weapon sets
  `ai.underFire`, the same rule the Tommy gun triggers through
  `weapon.sinceShot`.
- **Aim.** Rounds go down the centre of the view. From the hip the spread
  is 0.011 rad (plus 0.008 while moving). Holding RMB steadies: the gun
  rises to the eye's line in about 0.14 s, square, with the eye 1 cm above
  the sight line, so the top strap and rib run out to the front sight just
  under the centre (a six o'clock hold). The world lens narrows 7 degrees
  and the spread closes to 0.0012 rad. There is no crosshair.
- **Reload** (R, or a press on an empty gun), 1.7 s (strip `reload`). The
  eyes drop to the gun (a 0.17 rad dip; the head keeps what it holds) and
  the gun comes in with its left side up. The left hand comes in from the
  lower left, the cylinder swings out, the muzzle goes up and the palm
  strikes the ejector rod. The star comes back with the six cases on it.
  At 0.4 s they drop into the world's casings and ring on the stone. Muzzle
  down: the left hand comes up from the coat with six rounds, and three
  pairs go in (0.8, 1.02, 1.24 s) with the cylinder turned 120 degrees
  between pairs. A push shuts it (snap at 1.38 s), the gun comes back up
  and the thumb cocks. A press during the reload shuts the cylinder on
  what is in it (after the first pair, if none is in yet), brings the gun
  up and fires about 0.18 s later.
- **The count.** A small inked cylinder face sits bottom right while the
  gun is in hand (`hud.layer('revolver')`). Each chamber shows paper-white
  for a live round, a dark head in a grey ring for a spent one, and black
  for an empty one. The face turns with the cylinder and the notch at the
  top is the hammer. It fades out on the put-away and is dropped on
  unequip.
- **Grip poses for other lanes.** `revolverGripR` and `revolverGripL`
  (mirrored), the socket `revolver`, and `GRIP_F`
  (`src/player/clips/revolver.js`). B5 can hold it out of a car window
  with `hands.hold(buildProp('revolver'), 'L')` plus a clip keyed on
  `revolverGripL`.
- **The prop.** `buildProp('revolver')` is a four-inch .38: blued steel,
  walnut stocks with a medallion, a rib and a half-moon front sight, a
  grooved top-strap rear sight, the ejector lug, the latch, and a hammer
  and trigger on pivots. The cylinder is fluted between its chambers and
  rides on a crane that swings out left; it has an ejector star and rod and
  six rounds (brass cases, lead noses). The API: `setOpen`, `setTurn`,
  `setHammer`, `setTrigger`, `setEject`, `setChambers`, `chamberAt`,
  `muzzle` / `frontSight` / `rearSight`, and `rest` for lying on a table.
  Held, it keeps a contour between its parts (`vmPartIds`: one viewmodel
  id per part). Also `buildProp('roundsHand')`, the left hand's loose
  rounds.

**The hands, less balloon.** `HandRig.buildHandGeometry` is rebuilt on the
same rig: the same bones, lengths and sockets, so every clip and grip
lands where it did. The wrist and the back of the hand are one loft. It
starts inside the cuff as the forearm's own ellipse and eases into a flat,
slightly wedge-shaped slab with an arched back, ending at a slanted
knuckle line. Each knuckle is a roll across the joint, a little proud of
the back. Each finger is three tapered boxes: a superellipse section with
a flat top, flat sides and a rounder pad beneath. Each box steps down a
hair at the joint, over a roll as tall as the phalanx before it, so a bent
finger closes over the roll with no gap. The fingertip is domed. The thumb
is squared, with a flatter ball. The palm's radial edge is still weighted
into the thumb's metacarpal (the web), so nothing stands between thumb and
index. The knuckle spheres, torus ticks and nails are gone. The lines now
come from the geometry: the gaps between fingers, the corners of a fist's
knuckles, the web and the cuff. The skin's two-value print now splits on
plane edges instead of wandering over balloons. The glove uses the same
construction at the leather's girth (x1.06).

**The light-up.** On its way to the lips, the left hand now takes the
cigarette down under the chin and brings it up from below; after letting
go, it drops straight out of the bottom of the frame. It used to sweep
across the view 17 cm from the eye (measured by scrubbing the clip in the
page: 1.7 to 2.05 s). Now it only touches the bottom edge (strip
`lightup`).

**The range** (`src/scenes/90_range.js`, id `range`). It is not in
`order.js`, so the story never reaches it. `?start=range` plays it and
`?scene=range&beat=ready|fight|done` frames it. On the block in the
street's composition there are three Hoods down the avenue. Each has
`wake: 'shot'`, `leash: 0` and `aim: 0.45`, and they fire back from where
they stand. Q draws, Q puts away, then the end card.

## Shared files

- `src/player/Hands.js` (B3's own by the brief, listed as shared in
  WAVE_B; all edits marked `[B3]`, all additive):
  - `pose3`: a set may pose its hands itself (`S.pose(H, side, out)`),
    after `still` and a clip's keys, before the rest pose.
  - A gun set with a `brush` hand lays the brush field on that hand.
  - `hold()`: a prop with `userData.vmPartIds` gets one viewmodel id per
    part (opt-in; every other prop still gets one id).
  - `this.sockets` is exposed, as `this.poses` is, for fitting in the
    page.
- `src/player/HandRig.js` (B3's own): the new surface. It replaces the
  old geometry helpers and `buildHandGeometry`; the forearm pieces are
  untouched.
- `src/player/clips/diner.js`: two keys in `lightUp`'s left hand (1.66 and
  2.04), marked `[B3]`. The clip's duration and marks are unchanged.
- `src/player/weapons/revolver.js`: the insides replaced, the front kept
  (`REVOLVER` export, `name`, `set`, `ammo`, `mag`, `trigger`, `fire`,
  `reload`, `update`, `equip`, `unequip`, plus `holster()`).

No edit to Game.js, Hud.js, Controller.js, Weapons.js, HoodAI.js, the
render pipeline or the shaders.
