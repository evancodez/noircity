## The rules on the screen (the quiet pass)

1. **Light is being seen.** A man sees me if I stand in a lamp's pool
   anywhere in his view (60 degrees each side, 30 m), with a clear line.
   My hands print the light on me (as built).
2. **A man sees what his light shows.** His torch's or lantern's cone on me
   is seen; out of it and out of the lamps, only within 1.9 m in front of
   him (`TUNE.nearDark`, was 3.0: the brief's "arm's length").
3. **Running is heard (8 m), walking near (3 m), creeping never** (C toggles,
   Ctrl held - the rulebook's two ways to get low).
4. **They show what they think**: calm (the round), "hm" and the head then
   the light turning (curious, at least a second before he is sure), the
   walk to look (search), the shout. No meters.
5. **From behind, unseen: E** (as built: the step in, the blow, he folds).
   A body in a lamp's pool is found from across the floor (18 m); in the
   dark only in a beam (9 m) or at 2.5 m.
6. **Caught is a chase.** The shout ("They've seen me." as the kit's
   warning) and they run to where I was seen (not to me: out of every
   man's sight 3.2 s and they lose me, search there and settle - more
   watchful: their certainty 22 % quicker per scare, twice at most). The
   cosh only from a man within 1.45 m with a line on me, never sooner than
   1.4 s after the shout; then `game.fail({ why: 'They caught you.', to: <checkpoint> })`.
   **The men I put down stay down** through the retry. A checkpoint is
   never taken while a man is on to me (it waits for the calm).
7. **Waiting is not a way in**: the first man's round ends looking down his
   aisle at the side door; a player who stands in the dark by the door is
   found in about 35 s (pier_idle).
