## 2. The mapping (WAVE_C §2 on the pad)

One pad, one meaning; the layer reads what is on screen (`pad.context()`:
`title`, `pause`, `end`, `stills`, `play`, `brawl`, `drive`, `table`, `idle`)
and turns the pad into the keys and buttons the game already reads.

| Pad | Key / mouse | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| left stick | W A S D (and an analogue move: the walk as fast as it is pushed) | move; in a menu, the highlight |
| right stick | mouse moves (curve: 0.28 u + 0.72 u^2.4; 3.2 rad/s yaw, 2.0 pitch at full tilt, times the stick setting) | look; on a puzzle's table, the cursor |
| R2 | LMB | fire, rub, throw, the ash; **the right fist in a fist fight** |
| L2 | RMB | steady / rise over cover, turn a piece; **the left fist in a fist fight** |
| ✕ | SPACE | block, vault, jump, handbrake; in a menu, choose; on the stills, next |
| ○ | C (a tap toggles at once; held past 0.3 s, letting go stands again where C is a toggle) | get low, slide |
| □ | E | use / take / open |
| △ | R | reload |
| L1 (or L3) | Shift | run |
| R1 | F | smoke |
| d-pad ◄ ► ▲ / ▼ | Q / M | the room toys and the horn / sound; in a menu, the highlight |
| options | ESC | pause (and back out of it); on the stills, twice (or held) skips |
| touchpad, share, PS | nothing | |

By context:
- **The fist fight** (`brawl`): each trigger is the fist of its side - L2
  the left fist (the game's LMB), R2 the right (RMB). *The brief's table had
  R2 = LMB everywhere; in the fight LMB is the left arm (Brawl.js:
  `code === 'Mouse0' ? 'L' : 'R'`), so R2 would have thrown the left fist and
  the opening cue's "the fist on its side" would have been the wrong trigger.
  The brief itself says "L2: the left fist".* A flick of the left stick left
  or right is the dodge (A / D), back is the hop back, forward steps in; ✕
  held blocks; □ takes what is in reach.
- **The car** (`drive`): R2 the throttle and L2 the brake / reverse as far
  as they are pressed, the left stick the wheel as far as it is pushed
  (`pad.car`, read by `modes/drive.js`; a slight curve, |x|^1.3, for the
  fine corrections), ✕ the handbrake, L1 held leans out of the window to aim
  (RMB), **R1 fires (LMB) with R2 still on the throttle**, △ reloads, the
  right stick aims, □ gets out, the d-pad is the horn. *The brief's candidate
  was R2 fires while leaning and the throttle is let go; played by the good
  hands (with the throttle kept at what it was as L1 went down) he got away
  on the river road three tries in a row: a leaning player who had eased off
  could not close the gap without putting the gun away. The keyboard has no
  such trade-off (W stays held under RMB + LMB), and R1 under the index
  finger with R2 under the middle one is the usual drive-by hand.*
- **A puzzle's table** (`table`: the pad, the safe's dial, the torn check,
  and an inspected thing): both sticks move the game's own cursor (900 mouse
  counts a second at full tilt, curve 0.3 u + 0.7 u^2.2: slow and exact near
  the middle for the dial's numbers), R2 holds as LMB, L2 as RMB, L1 / R1
  turn a piece as the wheel does (900 units a second), □ is E (tear, the
  handle, step back).
- **Menus** (`title`, `pause`, `end`): the d-pad or the left stick moves an
  ink bracket (a held direction repeats after 0.4 s, then every 0.15 s), ✕
  chooses, ○ or options goes back (the table of contents shuts; the pause
  card resumes). A choice is the same press a click makes on that element,
  so the Hud's own handler runs.
- **The stills**: ✕ (or □) is the click; options is ESC (twice skips; held,
  its repeats skip as a held ESC does).
- A button held across a change of context is ignored until it is let go:
  the ✕ that chose BEGIN never reaches the stills, the options that paused
  never resumes, a trigger held into a fist fight does not throw a punch.
- Every press the layer emits lasts at least 50 ms (a fixed step must see it
  held: the gun mode's crouch toggle reads the key held, not the event).

## 3. The pointer lock: the pad holds the game

A pad has no click to give the browser (a gamepad press is no user
activation in Chrome), so it cannot ask for the pointer lock. Once the pad
starts the game (✕ on the title), resumes it (✕ on the pause card), restarts
it (✕ on THE END) or is used in play, `pad.holds` is true while the game is
playing and no card is up, and the game treats that as locked: `main.js`'s
`unlocked` (the pause behind the card), `Game.bindInput`'s `locked()` (the
keys' edges, the mouse buttons, the Tommy gun's trigger, the look), the
controller's `enabled`. The cursor is hidden over the frame (`body.padheld`).
Options pauses: with the real lock, the page lets it go
(`exitPointerLock`: the old path shows the card); without, the card is shown
directly. ✕ on RESUME hides it and asks for the lock again (Chrome grants it
without a gesture when the page itself let it go; refused, the pad holds the
game as before, and the refusal is not a pause). A real ESC while the pad
holds without the lock pauses (there is no lock for the browser to take). A
click on the frame takes the mouse back for a player who goes back to it. A
pad that disconnects while it holds the game without the lock pauses it.
The sound still needs one click or key (the browser's rule): the title's
sound line says `click once: sound` while the pad is the device.

## 4. The glyphs

`src/input/glyphs.js` draws them on canvases in the kit's ink, drawn at the
cap's size (44) and scaled: the face buttons as paper discs with an ink skirt
and rim and the shape brushed in; the shoulders as paper tabs lettered L1 /
R1, the triggers with a bowed top (L2 / R2); a stick as a disc with a ring,
its letter and, for a direction, an inked arrow at the rim; options as a pill
with its three lines; the d-pad as a cross with the pressed arm inked. One
table maps a key token to a glyph by context (`glyphOf`): `[SPACE]` ✕,
`[E]` □, `[C]` / `[CTRL]` ○, `[R]` △, `[SHIFT]` L1, `[F]` R1, `[Q]` d-pad,
`[ESC]` options, `[LMB]` R2 (fist fight: L2; car: R1), `[RMB]` L2 (fist
fight: R2; car: L1), `[W]` / `[A]` / `[S]` / `[D]` the left stick's
directions (car: `[W]` R2, `[S]` L2), `MOUSE` the right stick. Drawn for the
last device (`input.last`, set by any pad control past 0.3, and back to
`keys` by a real key, click or mouse move): the prompt's key, the teach card
(redrawn if the device changes while it is up), the fight's lesson cards
(and 'LEFT CLICK' / 'RIGHT CLICK' said as L2 / R2), the stills' hint, the
rulebook's keys line on the title and the pause card, the pause and end
cards' lines. Shot mode never attaches the pad: the golden frames see none
of it.

## 5. Rumble (small, honest, never continuous)

`gamepad.vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble')` where the pad has it,
one effect at a time at most every 50 ms, only while the pad is the device,
off in the pause card's settings (`rumble on / off`, kept in
`localStorage['noir.rumble']`; turning it on gives one short buzz): a kick on
every shot I fire (`shot`, 70 ms), a heavy one when I am hit (`player:hit`,
170 ms and more with the damage), a thud on a punch landing (`brawl:hit` by
me, head or a broken guard) and a heavier one taken, a light one on a blow
blocked, a soft pulse on the fist fight's dodge cue (`brawl:cue`), a buzz
on the car's jolts (its `shake` rising: a scrape, a hit, a kerb).
`pad.rumbles` keeps the last forty (the plays read it).

## 6. Proof without a pad: the fake pad and the play tool

- `?padfake=1`: `Pad.js` reads `window.__pad = { buttons: [18 values],
  axes: [4], rs?: { x, y, secs } }` instead of `navigator.getGamepads()`;
  `rs` holds the right stick for exactly `secs` (the look integrates that
  long: a turn of a known size).
- `tools/play.mjs`: the step `{ "pad": { "b": [names], "v": { "r2": 0.5 },
  "a": [lx, ly, rx, ry], "secs", "hold", "release" } }` (names: x o sq tri
  l1 r1 l2 r2 share opt l3 r3 up down left right ps tp), and the play key
  `"input": "pad"`: every key, mouse button, turn and wheel the steps and
  the drivers (`tools/lib/*.mjs`: the fight's, the tail's, the office's, the
  door's) ask for is played on the fake pad instead, by the inverse of the
  layer's mapping for what is on screen (`N.pad.context()`): a fist fight's
  Mouse0 is L2, the car's W is R2 and its RMB L1, a puzzle's wheel is R1 / L1
  held for as long as the units take, a turn is the right stick tilted and
  held for as long as the turn takes (the page's own curve and deadzone,
  `N.pad.cfg`). The keyboard and the mouse are never used: a pad play FAILs
  if one raw key or mouse event is sent (and a `clickOn` in a pad play is a
  FAIL), and gives the page no user gesture at all (a pad press is none).
