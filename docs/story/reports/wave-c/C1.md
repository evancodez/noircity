## What a player feels

- **BEGIN** opens on three comic panels over black (the tip, his men at my
  diner with Dot's white apron between them, the neon through a wet
  windscreen), landing one after another on one page, each with a soft paper
  sound; a click, E, SPACE or Enter moves on (never within 0.4 s of a panel
  landing), ESC twice skips (the hint at the page's foot says so). Then the
  diner comes up out of the black under card I as before. CONTINUE, CHAPTERS
  and `?start=` play no stills. Under a real pointer lock the browser's own
  ESC counts as the first press, and after the skip the diner comes up with
  a quiet "click to take the mouse" instead of the pause card.
- **An objective line**, top left, true for the beat I am in, in every
  chapter: brushed in with a dry-brush sound, full strength for 2.5 s, then
  quiet; a changed goal strikes the old line through first. The diner's
  (`Light up.` struck through when it is lit, `Watch the room.`, `Order a
  drink.`, `Drink. Watch the big man.`, `Pay the tab.`); every other chapter
  a stand-in per beat from `order.js` (VIII `Take the gun.` then `Hold the
  diner.`, III `Open the safe.` at the safe, VI `Make him talk.` at the
  water, VII `Put him down.` in the fist fight...), taken down in a beat
  that has none, until its lane declares its own.
- **The warning before going down**: in a gunfight, health falling under 45
  with a hit puts up "You're hit. Get to cover." low in the frame (under what
  I aim at); nobody goes down before a warning has been up a second (IV hands
  off: warned 7 s before the fall; VIII: 4 s).
- **The guns are taught**: IV shows `[LMB] fire` then `[C] get low` as the
  revolver comes out before the door goes in; VIII `[LMB] fire` as the Tommy
  gun is taken (once per save). The title and the pause card list the
  rulebook's keys (fire, steady, block, vault, dodge, get low, reload, run).
- **The end of II** shows Ames's card, crossed through in red (`AMES.
  MARKED.`), before III asks me to find him.
- **Mack's line** is now about her, not me: "She's got nothing to say to
  you." (WAVE_C §6 I; the slice play checks it).
- **The chapters** are eight (The Body is cut); II ends on Ames's card and
  goes straight into III Room 304 with the matchbook and the card in the coat.
- **A loss** (`game.fail`) is a short fall or a hard stop, black, a few
  hand-lettered words on the black, and back at the beat: 2.4 to 3.3 s to
  control. Going down to health zero anywhere in the story now does this
  (it was THE END card with "click to try again").
- **The music** follows one rule (MUSIC.md "The rule"): a CONTINUE into any
  beat cues that beat's slot; the title stops the end's music; the foreman's
  fist fight at Pier Nine now plays the `fight` slot.
