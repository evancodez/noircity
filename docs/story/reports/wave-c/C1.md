## What a player feels

- **BEGIN** opens on three comic panels over black (the tip, his men at my
  diner, the neon through a wet windscreen), landing one after another on one
  page, each with a soft paper sound; a click, E, SPACE or Enter moves on
  (never within 0.4 s of a panel landing), ESC twice skips. Then the diner
  comes up out of the black under card I as before. CONTINUE, CHAPTERS and
  `?start=` play no stills.
- **An objective line**, top left, from the first second of every chapter:
  brushed in with a dry-brush sound, full strength for 2.5 s, then quiet; a
  changed goal strikes the old line through first. The diner has one per
  beat (`Light up. Watch the room.`, `Order a drink.`, `Drink. Watch the big
  man.`, `Pay the tab.`); every other chapter shows its stand-in from
  `order.js` until its lane declares its own.
- **Mack's line** is now about her, not me: "She's got nothing to say to
  you." (WAVE_C §6 I; the slice play checks it).
- **The chapters** are eight (The Body is cut); II ends straight into III
  Room 304 with the matchbook and Ames's card in the coat.
- **A loss** (`game.fail`) is a short fall or a hard stop, black, a few
  hand-lettered words on the black, and back at the beat: 2.4 to 3.3 s to
  control. Going down to health zero anywhere in the story now does this
  (it was THE END card with "click to try again").
- **The music** follows one rule (MUSIC.md "The rule"): a CONTINUE into any
  beat cues that beat's slot; the title stops the end's music; the foreman's
  fist fight at Pier Nine now plays the `fight` slot.
