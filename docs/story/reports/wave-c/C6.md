**The run** (`player/modes/run.js`, rebuilt): **W runs** (0 to 6.8 m/s in about a
second), let go and I slow to a stop; S checks; A / D step aside; the mouse
steers; a hard turn at speed costs speed. Nothing goes over anything by itself:
- **SPACE goes over** (fences, the bins he pulls down, crates, the party wall,
  the skylight, the market's crates, the car's bonnet), **C / Ctrl goes under**
  (the fence broken at its foot, the half-shut roller door, the tank on its
  legs, the truck's plank, the barrow), **SPACE through** a door he slammed
  (E opens it from standing), **SPACE jumps** the light well between the
  roofs, **C slides down** the shed's roof.
- Timed: the press is judged by the time to reach the thing (`TIMING` in
  run.js: clean if 0.05 .. 0.42 s; a press is remembered 0.15 s, so one up to
  0.57 s early still comes good; earlier than that it is a hop or a slide on
  open ground that spends itself before the thing). Clean: over or under at
  speed (the vault 0.36 - 0.62 s, the palms on the top rail; the slide 0.68 s,
  the eye at 0.6 m). A little late: a scramble or a duck, about half a second.
  Not at all: a low thing trips me over it (0.85 s, the hands out, ~1.1 s
  lost); a tall one stops me dead, the hands on it, and W does nothing until I
  climb it from standing (SPACE, 0.7 - 0.95 s), crawl under it (C) or push its
  door (SPACE / E). The jump: early, late or from standing, the hands catch the
  far parapet and haul me up (~1.4 s lost) - never a fall.
- The input's edge is read (`game.pressed`), not only the key held at a step:
  a tap shorter than a step, or under a teach card's crawl, is never lost.
- Levels: feet over 1.2 m walk the stage's `roofColliders` (roofs, stair,
  parapets) instead of its `colliders` (the streets').

**Him** (`scenes/watcher/chase.js`): his own race on his way (`course.js`
`his(lane, over)`): 6.5 m/s on the flat (mine 6.8), slower into his corners
(down to 4.2 at 90 degrees), 5.0 up the stair (two treads at a time); his
fences (the vault clip at 1.25x, ~0.3 s lost each; for the low things - the
market's crates, the party wall - `watcher_lowvault`, new: a hand on the top
and over, 0.6 s), his slides (`watcher_slide`, new), the jump (an arc), the
shed (on his seat) and the drop; a look back over his shoulder five times (a
stride lost; not on the roofs when I took the row - no one behind him to look
back at); the one slip on the wet at the alley's corner. Close behind
him (under 7 m) a burst of +0.8 m/s while his breath lasts (3 s, back at 0.5/s
when I am over 9 m behind). **Nothing in his pace looks at how far behind I
am** beyond that fear: no rubber band. His doings: the alley's bins always (the
first SPACE); when I am under 9 m behind him as he passes them (and not ahead
of him), the alley's crates, the lane's bin kicked at me, the kitchen's rack,
the market's barrow.
He slams his door in the yard. He takes the yard's lanes at random each try
(the other door is locked); over, he always takes the roofs: a man on the
short way cannot be passed by one on the long, and the row is my choice.
On his heels I run at his pace, never through him (`capStep`).

**The rules** (`chase.js` `RULES`): the gap is the way between us along the
course (`course.js`: the shortest way on to the gate from where each of us
is; a man in the locked lane counts the way back out of it). Out of my sight
counts only past 18 m and not while we are on the two ways of the fork.
"He's getting away." at a gap of 24 m (off at 19) or 3.5 s unseen; "Lost him."
(`game.fail`) at 36 m or 7 s unseen, and only with the warning up a second;
back at the start of the stretch, him 12 - 14 m ahead (`STRETCHES[..].ahead`),
what he knocked down from that stretch on put back (`things.reset`). A
stretch's first 1.6 s after the black are a grace (no warning yet); under the
chapter card (a cold start) he and I are both held. Each "over" thing missed
costs ~5 m of gap: two or three missed in a stretch bring the warning, four
or five lose him.

**Teach cards** (seams/Z.md §2): `[W] run` (only if I stand 1.4 s at the
ladder's foot), `[SPACE] over` at the bins (held: time crawls until SPACE),
`[C] under` at the broken fence (held), `[SPACE] through it` at his door,
`[SPACE] jump` at the roof's edge, `[C] slide down` at the shed. Two fails in
a stretch: its card once more (`AGAIN` in 06_watcher.js).

**The objective lines** (declared, every beat): seen 'Check the street.',
escape 'After him. Down the fire escape.', alley .. street 'Don't lose him.',
car 'Get to your car.'. Music: `chase` from the umbrella's drop, declared for
every beat from escape on.
