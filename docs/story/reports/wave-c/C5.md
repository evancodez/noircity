### IV The Door (`src/scenes/05_gunfight.js`)
On the gun kit (seams/GUNS.md): `gunsOf(G).arm('revolver')` and `K.begin` in
every beat's setup (and in the script when III cuts straight in: `keep`
runs no setup). **The numbers go by my stance, every step** (`byStance`;
after the critic): down in cover they are the beat's - the door `settleK
1.2, dmgK 0.9`, the flank `2.0 / 0.8` (they come at me from two sides: the
time to turn), the corridor `1.25 / 0.9`, the heavy's rounds `0.55`; on my
feet, or down with nothing round me (the kit's `lowInOpen`), they are
`STANDING`: settle `0.85` (the difficulty table's floor) and rounds in full
(`1.0`). `maxFiring 2`. The door has the kit's flank (`flankAfter 5`, its
clock started at the kick); the flank and the corridor none. **A retry is a
little kinder only to a man in cover, and only from the second fall**
(`retryEase`: from the second fall at the beat, up to two steps, they settle
15 % slower and their rounds weigh 12 % less while I am down; on my feet,
none). Beats, each a checkpoint and a save: `shadows`, `flank`, `corridor`,
`quiet`. Lines: `Someone at the door. Get ready.` -> `Hold the room.` (the
door in) / `Hold the room.` / `Check the corridor.` (-> `One more. On the
stair.` as the heavy starts) / `See if more are coming.`

     the moment's start); from in front of it or its ends, a quick step round

     heave (my left palm on its edge), it goes over away from me toward the
     opening, the lamp thrown off burning on the floor; then C.
   - *Shoot through the glass*: the men are there; rounds go through the
     frosted glass and hit; a shot brings the door in at once.
   - *Move*: into the outer office (behind the secretary's desk), 305's
     doorway, the connecting door... Walking up to the door brings it in.
   - *Do nothing*: at the moment's end the door is kicked in.

   305 and the connecting door, and the movement waits for him), or 40 s.
2. **The flank** (the beat begins when the door is down to one): two men

   the door's last man (if one was left) is back at the door's jamb, working it
   as the jamb man did - put back where he stood, he could be three metres off
   by the opening with a line on me as the retry began.
3. **The corridor**: quiet. The lift is called when I step out of the
