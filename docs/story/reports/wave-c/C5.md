### III Room 304 (`src/scenes/04_office.js`, `scenes/office/search.js`, `pad.js`, `safe.js`, `check.js`)
Beats `corridor` (out of the lift, to his frosted door; the lock splintered,
I push it in, **it shuts behind me on its closer: the click, the glass
shivering in its frame**), `room` (through the opening: Ames dead in his
chair, the receiver off its hook turning on its cord), `check`, `done`
(`pad`, `safe` are cold-start beats only). Lines: `Room 304. Ames.` /
`Find what Ames had.` / `Open the safe.` (when the picture swings open) /
`Piece it together.` / `Pier 9.`. No fail.

The chain (any order; every link is a thing in the picture, not a prompt):
1. **The safe is found**: the pictures they got to are down on the floor,
   backs slit, pale patches where they hung; the harbour print over the safe
   stands a finger off the wall on its hinge - E, it swings open like a
   cupboard door.
2. **The lamp is off** at the start (the slats are the room's light). Its
   chain lights it; on the pad, faint dents show in its raking light (the
   pad's canvas embosses them when lit). The ashtray holds the burnt corner
   of the top sheet ('He burned the top sheet.').
3. **The key is on him**: E on Ames - my hand into his waistcoat (the key
   ring's jingle), then on his tie a moment; no words.
4. **The pencil is in his locked drawer** (top right; `locked` once tried,
   `unlock it` with the key). The desk's six drawers are real: shut, they
   run all the way out and back; one has his bottle and two glasses ('Two
   glasses. He had company.'), others are plainly empty, one was pulled out
   and emptied on the floor (its slot a black mouth). The cabinets' drawers
   the same (some left out by the crew).
5. **The pad** (lamp on, pencil in my pocket): the kit's card `[LMB] hold
   and rub` once; three numbers **big, white and whole** with **drawn curved
   arrows** (clockwise right, the other way left) come up as the sheet is
   shaded; the rest of the sheet shades too. No count ends it: once all
   three figures' rings are shaded (each 70 %), `E tear it off` - the player
   may go on rubbing first. Without the lamp: 'Too dark.'; without the
   pencil: 'Dents in it. Written hard.'
6. **The dial**: closer, a narrower lens, the figures every five (tens
   large), a white index wedge; the torn sheet held up beside it in the left
   hand (`review/02, 03`). Right - left - right as built; a wrong turn past
   resets that number only. E the handle.
7. **The envelope** in the safe: E takes it. On his desk, `lay them out`:
   the pad pushed aside, the lamp eased down, **the torn guest check** in
   seven pieces (lane C4's `drawGuestCheck` with `PIER 9 / MIDNIGHT / — DOT`
   in her pencil; three tears across between the words and the head, one
   down through each of the top bands). Unlit paper (it reads as paper under
   the lamp), legible while apart (`review/04`). LMB drags, the wheel or RMB
   turns, a piece within 9 mm and 14 degrees of where it tore from pulls home.
8. Whole: the stills `check` (`DOT'S HAND.` / `SHE SENT HIM TO THEM.`,
   lane C9's pictures when they come), `check` into the inventory,
   `flags.note = 'PIER 9'`, `Pier 9.` - and as I turn to go (a turn of the
   head, a step, or 6 s) the cut into IV in the same room, card IV small.

Flavour that says so quickly: his hat on the stand ('His hat. He never
left.'), the photograph on the cabinet ('France. He carried me out.'), the
telephone (hung up; lifted: 'Dead. They cut it.'), the cooler, the
typewriter, the dust sheets ('Nobody has moved in.'), the lift's button, the
radiator ('Stone cold.'), the cart, the washroom's chain and tap.

**A stall** (50 s with no step on, no puzzle open): a small hard light from
the ceiling flickers on the next thing in the chain (him; the lamp's chain;
the drawer; the pad; the picture; the dial; the envelope; the desk), for 2.6
s every 22 s - the room points, it never says (`office_review.json` checks it).

### IV The Door (`src/scenes/05_gunfight.js`)
On the gun kit (seams/GUNS.md): `gunsOf(G).arm('revolver')` and `K.begin` in
every beat's setup (and in the script when III cuts straight in: `keep`
runs no setup). Numbers: `maxFiring 2, settleK 1.2, dmgK 0.9`; the corridor
`settleK 1.4, dmgK 0.8`; `flankAfter 0` (each movement's way round is its
own). Beats, each a checkpoint and a save: `shadows`, `flank`, `corridor`,
`quiet`. Lines: `Someone at the door. Get ready.` -> `Hold the room.` (the
door in) / `Hold the room.` / `Check the corridor.` (-> `One more. On the
stair.` as the heavy starts) / `See if more are coming.`

1. **The pause** (first time): the music goes, a board in the corridor, the
   revolver out from under my arm; **the take**: the camera turned onto the
   frosted door across the outer office and the lens narrowed on it - three
   shapes on the door's glass and the sidelight, from their real joints
   (`review/05`) - and given back. Then the kit's cards in the quiet
   second: `[LMB] fire` (the revolver's first time in the story), `[C] get
   low`. **The moment**: 7.5 s (5 s on a retry, which skips the take).
   - *Tip the desk*: from its chair side, `tip the desk over` (E): the heave
     (my left palm on its edge), it goes over away from me toward the
     opening, the lamp thrown off burning on the floor; then C.
   - *Shoot through the glass*: the men are there; rounds go through the
     frosted glass and hit; a shot brings the door in at once.
   - *Move*: into the outer office (behind the secretary's desk), 305's
     doorway, the connecting door... Walking up to the door brings it in.
   - *Do nothing*: at the moment's end the door is kicked in.
   **Movement 1, the door**: the kicker through it (in to the outer office),
   one working it from the south jamb (gun up as it opens), the sidelight's
   glass knocked out and the third firing through its frame.
2. **The flank** (when the door is down to one): the connecting door's
   handle turns (`c5_handle`, the knob goes round, 0.75 s) - two men came
   up the corridor, through 305, and work it as a pair; the kit's warning
   `The other door! Behind you!`. A retry here keeps the desk as I left it
   and the door's last man where he was.
3. **The corridor**: quiet. The lift is called when I step out of the
   suite (or after 16 s): the dial sweeps up, the motor, the car rises into
   its cage, the bell, the cage folds open - **two men** (a pistol and a
   Tommy gun: the pin, the drum); when they are down, **the washroom's
   door** behind me (said as it opens); when he is down, a breath (5 s: a
   reload, a little mending) and **the gate below clanks**: the heavy up the
   stair (`One more. On the stair.`; 165 hp - three of my rounds, not five:
   the first gunfight's). Never more than two alive at once here. A stand-off
   (me holding the suite, the last man out there with no line on me for 15
   s - the Tommy gunner pins and never comes) sends him round to the suite's
   door to find me. Cover: the cart (north of 305's door), 305's doorway,
   the suite's doorway, the radiator; the outer office's desk.
4. **The quiet**: papers come down, `See if more are coming.`; at the
   street window, facing the blind, the cut into V (lane C6's first beat
   sets itself up at that window: its own E parts the slats). Not `keep`:
   kept, V's setup never ran and its script threw (`flags.chase`).

Bodies slump where they are (the kit's deaths); the dead are solid; the
desk lamp can be shot out by either side (their stray rounds no longer
spare it).
