### IV The Door (`src/scenes/05_gunfight.js`)
On the gun kit (seams/GUNS.md): `gunsOf(G).arm('revolver')` and `K.begin` in
every beat's setup (and in the script when III cuts straight in: `keep`
runs no setup). Numbers: `maxFiring 2, settleK 1.2, dmgK 0.9`; the flank
`settleK 1.5` (they come in at my side: the time to turn); the corridor
`settleK 1.25` (at 1.4 / dmgK 0.8 a player standing in the corridor trading
shots won it - too easy); `flankAfter 0` (each movement's way round is its
own). **A retry is a little kinder**: for each fall at the beat (up to two)
they settle 15 % slower and their rounds weigh 12 % less (`retryEase`); the
first try is as tuned. Without it the first-timer of WAVE_C §7 lost the
flank or the corridor three tries of three in half the runs. Beats, each a checkpoint and a save: `shadows`, `flank`, `corridor`,
`quiet`. Lines: `Someone at the door. Get ready.` -> `Hold the room.` (the
door in) / `Hold the room.` / `Check the corridor.` (-> `One more. On the
stair.` as the heavy starts) / `See if more are coming.`

1. **The pause** (first time): the music goes, a board in the corridor, the
   revolver out from under my arm; **the take**: the camera turned onto the
   frosted door across the outer office and the lens narrowed on it - three
   shapes on the door's glass and the sidelight, from their real joints
   (`review/05`) - and given back. Then the kit's cards in the quiet
   second: `[LMB] fire` (the revolver's first time in the story), `[C] get
   low`. **The moment**: 7.5 s (5 s on a retry, which skips the take).
   - *Tip the desk*: from its chair side, `tip the desk over` (E): the heave
     (my left palm on its edge), it goes over away from me toward the
     opening, the lamp thrown off burning on the floor; then C.
   - *Shoot through the glass*: the men are there; rounds go through the
     frosted glass and hit; a shot brings the door in at once.
   - *Move*: into the outer office (behind the secretary's desk), 305's
     doorway, the connecting door... Walking up to the door brings it in.
   - *Do nothing*: at the moment's end the door is kicked in.
   **Movement 1, the door**: the kicker through it (in to the outer office),
   one working it from the south jamb (gun up as it opens), the sidelight's
   glass knocked out and the third firing through its frame.
2. **The flank** (the beat begins when the door is down to one): two men
   set off from the corridor's south end once the door's last man is down
   (or 10 s on, if he hangs back - with him alive in the doorway the room
   has no place covered from both doors, and nearly all the good bot's and
   the first-timer's falls here were that), through 305, and the connecting
   door's handle turns (`c5_handle`, the knob goes round, 0.75 s): the
   first works it from its jamb; the second comes through it 2.5 s behind
   him (the pair side by side at the jambs lost the good bot its first try;
   held back until the first was down, the first's body in the doorway kept
   the second in his way in for good - 150 s of stand-off in play); the
   kit's warning `The other door! Behind you!`. A retry here keeps the desk as I left it;
   the door's last man (if one was left) is back at the door's jamb, working it
   as the jamb man did - put back where he stood, he could be three metres off
   by the opening with a line on me as the retry began.
3. **The corridor**: quiet. The lift is called when I step out of the
   suite (or after 16 s): the dial sweeps up, the motor, the car rises into
   its cage, the bell, the cage folds open - **two men** (a pistol and a
   Tommy gun: the pin, the drum); when they are down, **a door down the hall
   opens**: 306's, one more (working it from his jamb); when he is down, a
   breath (5 s: a reload, a little mending) and **the gate below clanks**: the
   heavy up the stair (`One more. On the stair.`; 165 hp - three of my rounds,
   not five: the first gunfight's; his rounds at `dmgK 0.55` once he starts -
   the kit heavy's bursts at nine a second had a good hand at the cart down
   2.3 s after the first round, and at 0.7 the first-timer met him three
   tries of three). Never more than two alive at once here,
   and all of it from the corridor's south end: the cart answers it. (The
   third man came out of the washroom first, behind me: the good hand down at
   the cart was flanked six tries of six.) A stand-off
   (me holding the suite, the last man out there with no line on me for 15
   s - the Tommy gunner pins and never comes) sends him round to the suite's
   door to find me. Cover: the cart, 305's doorway, the suite's doorway; or
   hold the outer office (the secretary's desk) - they come in at the door,
   and one goes round by 305 and the connecting door to the opening.
4. **The quiet**: papers come down, `See if more are coming.`; at the
   street window, facing the blind, the cut into V (lane C6's first beat
   sets itself up at that window: its own E parts the slats). Not `keep`:
   kept, V's setup never ran and its script threw (`flags.chase`).

Bodies slump where they are (the kit's deaths); the dead are solid; the
desk lamp can be shot out by either side (their stray rounds no longer
spare it).
