## Found and fixed (most felt first)

1. **IV (and anywhere glass breaks): shards frozen in the air, kept
   through every retry.** Steps: `?start=gunfight.flank`, fail (or be shot
   down) and retry; look at the sidelight and the desk. Each try left about
   105 shards (meshes with their ink lines) hanging where they were when the
   list lost them, never moving, never cleared by the retry (1029 -> 2275
   objects in the scene over ten retries; 44 already in the first try).
   Cause: `ShardField.update` (`src/destruct/Glass.js`) splits a big shard
   on a hard landing; the split's `spawn()` drops the oldest shard off the
   front of the list once past `SHARD_CAP`, so the loop's index pointed at a
   neighbour: `splice(i)` took that neighbour out of the list unkilled (an
   orphan in the scene for good) and left the killed shard in it. Fix: the
   split shard is found by `indexOf`, and the loop skips an index the shift
   ran past. After: 0 orphans; `gunfight.flank` 3 retries: 147 -> 149
   shards, all in the field.
2. **Every retry left the last try's things on the GPU** (the "does
   anything pile up" question of the brief: frame time did not move, the
   memory did). Found by `pile.mjs` with `HUNT_GEO` (the stack that made
   each geometry still uploaded):
   - **every man's bone texture** (`src/enemies/Hood.js` `dispose`): each
     Hood's skeleton has its own texture; a removed man kept his. Now
     `skeleton.dispose()` with him, and the things made for him (a torch, a
     hook, a lantern, his cigarette, the Cardinal's cane) go with him; his
     body's LODs and the hit shapes are shared caches and stay.
   - **VI: every car of the try** (`src/vehicles/index.js` `remove`,
     `src/vehicles/Vehicle.js` `Headlamps.dispose`): the traffic's and his
     sedan's own glass, lenses, contact shadows and tail lamps, and every
     car's headlamp cones and pool (+55 geometries a retry). A car's own
     geometries go when it is removed; those shared with a car still in the
     stage (the sedan's templates) stay; my coupe's model is kept for the
     next spawn as before. And the walkers' umbrellas
     (`src/scenes/tail/walkers.js`).
   - **VII: the bottles on the low crates, the photograph, the jacket**
     (`src/scenes/08_warehouse.js`, both places the scene lets its props go)
     and **the crew's torches and lanterns** (`src/scenes/warehouse/crew.js`
     `remove`).
   - **II: the counter set again** (`src/stages/block/parts/diner.js`
     `setTable`): every plate, cup, bottle and paper on the counter was
     rebuilt on a retry and the old ones kept (+24 a retry).
   - **VI: the revolver out of the window** (`src/scenes/tail/gun.js`
     `show`): built again each try, the last one taken off the viewmodel
     but kept on the GPU (+16 geometries a retry).
   Numbers in "Ten fails, before and after".
3. **VI's cars outlived the chapter** (`src/scenes/tail/chase.js`
   `dispose`): my coupe and his sedan stayed in `game.vehicles` through VII
   and VIII (the seam dump: `veh: 2` in the warehouse), holding two of the
   figures' 64 ink slots and the city's colliders. Now the try's cars go
   with the chase on `scene:exit` (the city still the stage then); a retry
   spawns them again as before.
4. **VII picked from CHAPTERS never taught the revolver** (section 2: "a
   chapter picked from CHAPTERS teaches what it needs if the save has not
   seen it"). A chapter picked starts a fresh save; IV's `[LMB] fire` and
   `[R] reload` were never shown before VII's loud pass. Now the loud pass

  steps the world straight through a fail's hard stop (the real loop holds

  wrote it while the fail was going back to `start`. By real time
  (`tailidle.mjs`) the beat and the save never move while I sit at the kerb.

  out of the car's door being resolved; no walk makes them.
- The green blob on the road in VI's first seconds is the green neon's
  reflection (lane C3c's three colours), not a debug marker.
- The fall of a fail (0.9 s) lets the world run on (only the stop holds it):
  nothing of theirs hits then (`game.live` is false) and nothing of mine
  fires (the edges hunt: 0 shots under the fall and the black).
