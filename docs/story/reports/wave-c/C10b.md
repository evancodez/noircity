## The whole play (section 1)

`tools/lib/whole.py` (rewritten for wave C; wave B's builder is parked as
`tools/plays/_wave_b/whole_wave_b.py`) writes `tools/plays/whole.json` (the
good hands) and `whole_first.json` (the clumsy ones) from the lanes' own
plays and drivers: the slice (the title, BEGIN, the opening stills, I, the
way into II), `fight_bot` (II), lane C5's office chain (III) and door
driver (IV, the desk), lane C6's chase (V), lane C3's tail (VI), lane C7's
steps (VII's quiet pass: `pier_ghost` / `pier_bad`; the office:
`pier_office`), its loud driver and the fight bot (the foreman), lane C8's
nick and block drivers (VIII), the walk out to the hat, THE END, the title
and BEGIN again. `python3 tools/lib/whole.py --from <chapter>` writes a play
from that chapter on (scratch, not committed). The gun drivers
(`c5_door.mjs`, `c8_nick.mjs`, `c8_block.mjs`) take `flow: true`: each beat
comes by the story and a retry by the game's own fail, never `scenes.go`;
`c6_chase.mjs` and `c7_pier.mjs` now count only their own fails (they
counted an earlier chapter's in one session). At each chapter's start the
play measures the page and checks what the chapter finds (the carry, the
hands, the mode); a recorder keeps the cards, the stills, the cues, the
teach cards in the order taught, the saves, the fails.
