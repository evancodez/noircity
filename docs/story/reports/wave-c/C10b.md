## Found and fixed (most felt first)

1. **VIII's saloon: on every retry his car made the door with no warning
   up** (WAVE_C §1 rule 2), and a first-timer could not get through it
   (lane C8's clumsy hand, in a play from the title: fourteen tries, then
   the play gave up). Steps: `?start=shootout.saloon`, let his car reach the
   door once (warned 6.5 s, 'His car's nearly at the door! The tyres!'),
   then again: from the retry on the warning never came. Cause: the beat's
   tick (`saloonBeat`, `09_shootout.js`) removed itself when `G.failing`
   was set, and a retry's car comes 0.5 s into the beat, still under the
   fail's black - so on every retry the tick that writes the warning (and
   that lets a car held up by a wreck stand where it is) was gone before it
   ran once. Fix: it waits the black out. Play:
   `tools/plays/c10b_saloon_probe.json` (the el by the good hand, then the
   saloon by the clumsy one, in one session, with a log of his car and the
   warning line every half second): before, try 4 'His car made the door.'
   warned 0.72 s; after, every fall warned 6.1 - 8.1 s and through on the
   fourth try (one tyre, both lamps). Lane C8's own proofs went to the
   saloon by `scenes.go` and lost there on the first try only, so they
   never met a retry's car.
2. **VIII's nick of time could not be won by the window in a play from the
   title after VII's loud pass** ('He heard you coming.' on every try, the
   man crouched). Steps: VII loud (or `?start=warehouse.loud`), win, walk
   to the coupe, in VIII go over Mack's sill and low up the aisle. Cause:
   VII's gun-mode footfall wrapper (`wrapSteps`, `08_warehouse.js`) was kept
   as the sneak mode's `prevStep` when the brawl gave way to the sneak, so

   it, and it reported each step to VIII's ears as a man on his feet. Fix:
   the wrapper is inert once its scene is gone, and the scene's exit takes
   it out of the sneak's `prevStep` too. Play:
   `tools/plays/c10b_seam_loud_nick.json` (before: 3 of 3 tries heard;
   after: won first try).
3. **VIII's retries piled geometry on the GPU** (the brief's "does anything
