## The cue: three designs, one kept

Drawn and compared on the same moment (his right hook at its commit, 1440 x
900; `02_cue_designs`): **ink** (kept) - the dark side a loaded brush laid on
from its edge, torn inner edge, bristles; the light side paper torn open, white
bristles, three brush strokes sliding toward it; the fist marked by three white
strokes trailing it from the dark side. **wash** (`?cue=wash`) - a soft ink
wash over the dark half and one big stroke to the light: in a dark room the
wash barely shows, and the stroke reads as a UI bar. **lines** (`?cue=lines`) -
speed lines from the dark edge converging on the fist: says where it comes
from, not where to go. Only the ink one says "go that way" without being told.
