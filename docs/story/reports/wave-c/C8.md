## The street's own rules (the gun kit's, used by the place)
- **Cover is spent**: the windows go pane by pane (their first rounds), the wall
  below the sills and the counter stop rounds, the open car doors do not.
- **The room's light** (E at the switch by the door): dark, the men outside
  cannot see me in the room until I fire (2.5 s after each round of mine) -
  `K.see` wrapped in `beginFight`.
- **Drums are few**: two come with the gun; E at the counter when I have none
  left: Dot comes up with one (`RESERVE` in `shootout/rig.js`: start 2, max 3,
  one a time).
- **A car shot long enough burns and goes** (world/Cars.js, as built): the men
  behind it die in it; near the Cardinal it knocks him down and opens him.
- **Nobody hides out the clock**: no round from any of them for 12 s, the one of
  the kit's men nearest me comes in to the kerb before the diner (`pressOn`).

## Cars never pass through cars
`shootout/rails.js` `ahead(car)`: every step a car on its rail sweeps the way
ahead of its nose (the car's width, out to its stopping distance) against every
collider (another car's body and its open doors, a wreck, a column, a post, a
hydrant, a living man, a body) and me; it brakes for the first thing in it and
stops short; held up 1.2 s, its rail's `onBlocked` says so (the drive-by: they
get out). A crash into a post stops against whatever it meets first. The
rails themselves are laid round the block's parked sedans, the el's columns
where they cross the road far down the avenue, and the lamp posts.
