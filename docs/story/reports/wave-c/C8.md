## The street's own rules (the gun kit's, used by the place)
- **Cover is spent**: the windows go pane by pane (their first rounds), the wall
  below the sills and the counter stop rounds, the open car doors do not.
- **The room's light** (E at the switch by the door): dark, the men outside
  cannot see me in the room until I fire (2.5 s after each round of mine) -
  `K.see` wrapped in `beginFight`.
- **Drums are few**: two come with the gun; E at the counter when I have none
  left: Dot comes up with one (`RESERVE` in `shootout/rig.js`: start 2, max 3,
  one a time).
- **A car shot long enough burns and goes** (world/Cars.js, as built): the men
  behind it die in it; near the Cardinal it knocks him down and opens him.
- **Nobody hides out the clock**: no round from any of them for 12 s, the one of
  the kit's men nearest me comes in to the kerb before the diner (`pressOn`).

## Cars never pass through cars
`shootout/rails.js` `ahead(car)`: every step a car on its rail sweeps the way
ahead of its nose (the car's width, out to its stopping distance) against every
collider (another car's body and its open doors, a wreck, a column, a post, a
hydrant, a living man, a body) and me; it brakes for the first thing in it and
stops short; held up 1.2 s, its rail's `onBlocked` says so (the drive-by: they
get out). A crash into a post stops against whatever it meets first. The
rails themselves are laid round the block's parked sedans, the el's columns
where they cross the road far down the avenue, and the lamp posts.

## Where the brief was changed by play (and why)
- **The drive back is cut**; the still `diner` stays (the brief allows it: the
  lane's fifth went to the nick of time and the street).
- **No kitchen.** The block has no kitchen behind the diner (the hatch is a
  painted backdrop) and building one - a door in the back wall, a room, a
  back passage to the alley - was more than the lane could afford well. Its
  two uses are given to what exists: the third way in is **Mack's window**
  (out since II: over the sill, as II's way out); the flank is **the man for
  Dot round the corner from the alley's mouth, up the pavement, in at the
  door** (the bell, *"Dot: The door!"*), and two more from the other end of
  the block down the north pavement.
- **"One second" at the door is two.** By real input a player who runs in
  (Shift) from the pulled door is on him in about 1.3 s; a walk is 2.2 s and
  shot. One second could not be met by the run either.
- **Nicky** (the brief's "another at the door looking out at the street")
  stands out under the awning, a step off the wall, looking across the road:
  he sees the road and the pavement before him, never the strip along the wall
  at his back; that strip is the way to him from behind.
- **A bottle into the road** rather than through the far window: thrown from
  the pavement past him, a far window is past Nicky's reach and the arc's;
  into the road he walks out to it, and Sal hears the glass of the street.
- **The hydrant** (his knock-down) is not on his way (the block's hydrants are
  30 m down the avenue): his openings are his shot, his shield man's fall and
  a car going up near him.
