## The car (src/vehicles/Car.js)

A bicycle model with tyres that have a peak and a slide past it, load
transfer (forward on the brake, back on the throttle), the friction circle
on each axle, a traction limit on the rear, speed-sensitive steering (the
lock narrows so a flick at 60 asks for about the grip), a steadying hand
(the yaw drawn back to what the wheels ask when the hands ease; never a spin
on its own), a handbrake that locks the rear. Numbers (`c3sim.mjs`):
- 0 to 30 km/h 2.5 s, 0 to 50 4.0 s, flat out 75 km/h; 50 to 0 in 1.6 s, 11 m.
- Full lock: 20 km/h a 5 m radius, 30 km/h 8.6 m (0.82 g), 40 to 60 km/h
  about 0.87 g. A city corner at the lane is a 25 to 35 km/h corner.
- The handbrake at 30 to 50 km/h with the wheel over: 35 to 40 degrees by
  0.6 s, a right angle by 1.1 s, 12 km/h left.
- Trail-braked into a corner at 55 and let go: the tail out 20 to 28 degrees,
  back straight on its own.
- Full throttle, full lock from 15 km/h: the tail out to 24 degrees, then
  back (the throttle bites less past the tyres' peak).
- Walls: a square blow at 30 km/h is half a damage step, at 45 one, at 60
  about two; a glance at 30 degrees a third and 45 km/h kept. Four steps is
  a wreck: a headlamp out (1), the bonnet up a little and steaming (2), the
  wheel pulling (3), wrecked (4). Mended at a checkpoint.
- Lamp posts give to a blow over 4.5 m/s (they bend over and go out, 20 %
  of the speed lost, damage by the blow); el columns, walls, fences, the rail
  do not. Parked cars shunt (heavier, on locked wheels) and rock.
- Contacts are impulses at the contact point (restitution 0.18, friction
  0.28 on walls, 0.35 car on car): a glance turns the car along the wall, a
  square hit stops it.

The jank drive, headless: `node tools/lib/c3jank.mjs` - my car at 20 and
60 km/h into a building's wall, an el column, a lamp post, a parked car, the
river's rail, the yard fence, the canal's rail, the bridge's barrier, the
depot's wall, the pier's gate, the roadworks, a car head-on and one from
behind; W two seconds into it, then S. Each checked: never more than a hand

never faster than flat out, no spin, nothing NaN. All 26 pass. (Two fixes
from it: a face met flat - two corners as deep - is met at its middle, so a
square blow never spins the car on one corner; and standing on the brake
against a thing is a stop, not a wedge - the unsticking pivot only turns a
car that is asked to go.)
