## 1. The pocket

Before: R reloaded the revolver from an endless pocket (WAVE_C §1: a cheat),
and every checkpoint gave the cylinder back full whatever was spent.

Now (`src/player/weapons/revolver.js`):

- **`POCKET`**, one table, what each chapter gives:

  | chapter | gives | a retry (a checkpoint) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | IV The Door (`gunfight`) | the cylinder full and **24** in the pocket | the counts as they were when the checkpoint was reached, never under 6 + 18 |
  | VI The Tail (`tail`), the gun phase | the cylinder and **6** (the last rounds) | the river road's start: 6 + 6 again |
  | VII Pier Nine (`warehouse`) | **empty** (0 + 0) until the shells: the box gives 6 + 18 | the loud pass: as reached, never under 6 + 18 |

  A scene not in the table (the test beds `range`, `guns`, `kit`) keeps an
  endless pocket (`pocket: Infinity`, no ticks drawn).
- **`weapons.revolver.pocket`**, the rounds in reserve. `W.setCounts({ rounds,
  pocket })`, `W.armCounts(opts)`, `W.keep()`, `W.mark(key)`.
- **The scenes read it through `gunsOf(G).arm('revolver', opts)`**
  (`src/guns/Guns.js`, docs/story/seams/GUNS.md's call, now with opts):
  `arm('revolver')` in a beat's setup gives the checkpoint's counts (or the
  chapter's row when it is begun); `{ pocket: n }` the cylinder full and n;
  `{ keep: true }` the counts as they are (VII's box reload and the loud
  pass's first second use it: a round fired in the office stays fired).
- **A reload takes from the pocket, and only fills the empty chambers**: the
  spent cases go out, the live rounds stay in (they used to be dumped and
  six new ones taken from nowhere); the feed starts at the first chamber
  after the live run, so old and new rounds go round one after another
  (no dry click mid-cylinder); the left hand comes up with as many rounds as
  it will load; once it is empty the rest of the feed motion runs at three
  times (a partial reload is quicker: the full one is 1.7 s, four rounds
  about 1.4 s). A press during it still shuts on what is in; each pair costs
  what it fed.
- **The pocket empty**: R loads nothing; a press on an empty cylinder is the
  hammer on nothing (the dry click the pier has, `revDry`) and the HUD says
  `Empty.` once (again only after the gun has been loaded again).
- **The count beside the cylinder** (`src/player/weapons/_revolverHud.js`
  `drawPocket`): the pocket as rows of six little inked cartridges left of the
  cylinder face (a row is a reload's worth; up to four rows), the top row the
  one being spent - its gone rounds greyed hollow; an empty pocket is one row
  all hollow. Hidden with the gun (the layer goes when it is put away; it
  fades with the holster).
- **The tail's window gun** (`src/scenes/tail/gun.js`) takes its pocket from
  `POCKET.tail` (it was 6 + 6 written in; the balance the tail's proofs have
  is kept) and now shows the same cylinder face and cartridges while the gun
  is out of the window or reloading (not in shot mode without `hud=1`); a
  press on an empty gun with nothing in the pocket says `Empty.` once.
- **Saves carry the counts**: `save.flags.ammo = { r, p, at: { 'scene.beat':
  [r, p] } }`, kept as they change and marked at every beat of a chapter in
  the table (`scene:beat`). A CONTINUE or a retry at a checkpoint gives back
  `at[scene.beat]`, floored; a chapter picked from CHAPTERS starts with empty
  flags and so gets its row.
