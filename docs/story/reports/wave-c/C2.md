## What there is

- **The test bed**: the stage `killhouse` (`src/stages/killhouse/`, salt
  9191) and the scene `guns` (`src/scenes/91_guns.js`, `?start=guns`,
  `?start=guns.<beat>`, `?scene=guns&beat=<beat>`; not in the order). Two
  rooms (A: the office with a desk that tips; B: the bar with a counter, a
  back bar of bottles, crates, barrels) and a corridor between them with real
  doors (A1, B1; the back door BD at its end, locked until they come), the
  lobby with a reception counter, a frosted glass partition with a glazed
  door (B2) into the bar, the street door (SD); outside, a short wet street
  with two parked sedans, crates, a hydrant, a warehouse wall with a loading
  door, barricades at the street's ends. Lit by pendants (pools in the dark)
  and two gooseneck lamps outside.
- **The beats**: `ready` (the teach), `door`, `two_ways`, `pin`, `rush`,
  `street` (Tommy gun), `street_rev` (revolver), `done`. Each is a contest
  with its own checkpoint.
- **The kit** (`src/guns/`): `Guns.js` (the encounter, the rounds as rays,
  the cover kinds, the player's and their rounds, the feedback, the fall's
  reason, the director: fire tokens and the flank), `Covers.js` (the men's
  cover points and fire spots from the stage), `deaths.js` (the fall by what
  is beside him), `Bodies.js` (`game.bodies`), `kitStub.js` (lane C1's kit
  until it merges).
- **The men** (`src/enemies/brains/gunman.js`): pistol, tommy, rusher; the
  tell, the notice, the settle; cover to cover; the doorway; the flank.
- **The mode** `gun` (`src/player/modes/gun.js`).
- **The clips** (`src/enemies/gunClips.js`): kneel; deathSlump,
  deathSlumpWall, deathSlumpOver.
- **The sounds** (`src/audio/sounds/c2_guns.js`).
