# C2 — the gun kit (report and handover)

Branch `wave-c/C2-guns`, port 5342. Brief: `docs/story/briefs/C2_GUNS.md`.
The contract for the chapters that use it: `docs/story/seams/GUNS.md` (read
that, and play `?start=guns`; nothing else is needed).

## What there is

- **The test bed**: the stage `killhouse` (`src/stages/killhouse/`, salt
  9191) and the scene `guns` (`src/scenes/91_guns.js`, `?start=guns`,
  `?start=guns.<beat>`, `?scene=guns&beat=<beat>`; not in the order). Two
  rooms (A: the office with a desk that tips; B: the bar with a counter, a
  back bar of bottles, crates, barrels) and a corridor between them with real
  doors (A1, B1; the back door BD at its end, locked until they come), the
  lobby with a reception counter, a frosted glass partition with a glazed
  door (B2) into the bar, the street door (SD); outside, a short wet street
  with four parked sedans, crates, a hydrant, a warehouse wall across the
  way with a loading door and two alley mouths (dark recesses behind them),
  board fences closing the street's ends. Lit by pendants (pools in the
  dark) and two gooseneck lamps outside.
- **The beats**: `ready` (the teach: tip the desk, low, rise, two bottles,
  reload down), `door` (two through one door, working it), `two_ways` (two
  through one door, one through the other), `pin` (a Tommy gunner working
  the glazed door, a pistol man), `rush` (the heavy in at the street door, a
  pistol man later), `street` (Tommy gun: three men down behind the far
  cars when it opens, a fourth out of the loading door; I start down behind
  the near car, the street door locked behind me), `street_rev` (the same,
  revolver), `done`; `lab` (one man, one death, for the strips:
  `&lab=wall|over|door|open|kneel`). Each contest has its own checkpoint.
- **The kit** (`src/guns/`): `Guns.js` (the encounter, the rounds as rays
  through the cover kinds, the player's and their rounds, the feedback, the
  fall's reason, the director: fire tokens and the flank), `Covers.js` (the
  men's cover points and fire spots from the stage), `deaths.js` (the fall
  by what is beside him), `Bodies.js` (`game.bodies`), `kitStub.js` (lane
  C1's kit until it merges).
- **The men** (`src/enemies/brains/gunman.js`, numbers in `GUNMAN`):
  pistol, tommy, rusher; the tell, the notice, the settle (the brief's 0.4 -
  0.9 s); cover to cover; the doorway worked from beside the jamb; going out
  together; the push, the seek, the flank (the last man too); the heavy who
  walks at me aiming and firing.
- **The mode** `gun` (`src/player/modes/gun.js`): C / Ctrl crouch, RMB rise
  over (re-judged as the view turns), lean round tall cover.
- **The clips** (`src/enemies/gunClips.js`): kneel; deathSlump,
  deathSlumpWall, deathSlumpOver, deathSlumpOverHi.
- **The sounds** (`src/audio/sounds/c2_guns.js`): the crack past the ear,
  the hit, the cock and the bolt (the tell), magazine and drum, wood hit and
  break, the doors, the desk's fall, the flanker's run.

## Left (most felt first)

1. **Not yet played by a person.** Everything above is bots and frames:
   the rise's speed, the lean, how readable the tell is at a distance and
   in the dark, whether the crack past the ear tells you where - all
   unjudged by a hand on the mouse.
2. **The lean round tall cover** is built (`gun.js`) but no play uses it
   (the good bot only rises over).
3. **The pin is slow for the good bot** (75 - 100 s): the Tommy gunner
   works the glazed door from behind the frosted partition and the bot
   never shoots a shadow through frosted glass (it only shoots what it can
   see). A person would; that path (my rounds through frosted glass and a
   thin door, x0.85 / x0.62) is built and probed but not proven by a play.
4. **`door` is won by the bad play** every run (two men through one
   doorway are shot as they come: the doorway's exposure, as the brief
   wants); its other wins vary run to run (during tuning it lost 4 - 5 of 6).
5. **The good bot's revolver lands 79 %** (the brief: 60 - 70 %), the Tommy
   gun 35 %: 61 % overall. Turning the hand's error up past 0.29 m tipped it
   to losing (0.4 m: 46 % and three beats lost).
6. **The street's men are there when it opens** (`cover: true`), not
   walking in: men who ran out of the openings were mown down by a standing
   Tommy gun before they could do anything. Only the fourth walks in.
7. **A man seeking a way in at me paths through doors** (the nav grid has
   them open). A locked door between a man and the player (the street door
   in the test bed, locked behind me) could hold a seeking man at it; the
   test bed never puts me behind it in a contest.
8. **The kneel, the Tommy gunner's exposed drum change, the heavy's walk
   with the gun up**: checked in frames and strips only (review below), not
   in motion by eye.
9. **`game.bodies`** is wired and probed (add / free / steer / remove) but
   nothing in the test bed steers with it (the gunmen use the Hood brain's
   path following and the kit's `resolveMan`); C4 is its first user.
10. **Looks**: grey but whole, as ordered - no art pass on the killhouse,
    the kit's stand-in HUD lines (C1's will replace them), the frosted
    shadows simple.
