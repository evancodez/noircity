# C2 — the gun kit (report and handover)

Branch `wave-c/C2-guns`, port 5342. Brief: `docs/story/briefs/C2_GUNS.md`.
The contract for the chapters that use it: `docs/story/seams/GUNS.md` (read
that, and play `?start=guns`; nothing else is needed).

Two owners. The first built the lane (grey but whole, the proofs passing);
the second (this report's last pass) took it on cold: merged lane C1's kit
in, played it, and worked on what a first-time player would feel - the
kit's lines and cards, the men and the doors, the danger and its warning,
the place's look, the proofs made honest and wider. "Second pass" below is
what changed then.

## What there is

- **The test bed**: the stage `killhouse` (`src/stages/killhouse/`, salt
  9191) and the scene `guns` (`src/scenes/91_guns.js`, `?start=guns`,
  `?start=guns.<beat>`, `?scene=guns&beat=<beat>`; not in the order). Two
  rooms (A: the office with a desk that tips; B: the bar with a counter, a
  back bar of bottles, crates, barrels) and a corridor between them with real
  doors (A1, B1; the back door BD at its end, locked until they come), the
  lobby with a reception counter, a frosted glass partition with a glazed
  door (B2) into the bar, the street door (SD); outside, a short wet street
  with four parked sedans, crates, a hydrant, a warehouse across the way with
  a loading door and two alley mouths (dark recesses behind them, a lamp over
  each), the city's towers over its parapet, board fences closing the
  street's ends. Lit by pendants (pools in the dark), a lit corridor, two
  gooseneck lamps and the wall lamps outside.
- **The beats**: `ready` (the teach: tip the desk, get behind it, low, rise,
  two bottles, reload down), `door` (two through one door, working it),
  `two_ways` (two through one door, one through the other), `pin` (a Tommy
  gunner working the glazed door, a pistol man), `rush` (the heavy in at the
  street door, a pistol man later), `street` (Tommy gun: three men down
  behind the far cars when it opens, a fourth out of the loading door; I
  start down behind the near car, the street door locked behind me),
  `street_rev` (the same, revolver), `done`; `lab` (one man, one death, for
  the strips: `&lab=wall|over|door|open|kneel`). Each contest has its own
  checkpoint and its own objective line.
- **The kit** (`src/guns/`): `Guns.js` (the encounter, the rounds as rays
  through the cover kinds, the player's and their rounds, the feedback, the
  fall's reason, the director: fire tokens and the flank, the teach cards,
  the warning), `Covers.js` (the men's nav grid and cover points from the
  stage, the doors in it), `deaths.js` (the fall by what is beside him),
  `Bodies.js` (`game.bodies`).
- **The men** (`src/enemies/brains/gunman.js`, numbers in `GUNMAN`):
  pistol, tommy, rusher; the tell, the notice, the settle (the brief's 0.4 -
  0.9 s); cover to cover; the doorway worked from beside the jamb; going out
  together; the push, the seek, the flank (the last man too); the heavy who
  walks at me aiming and firing; any man through the doors on his way.
- **The mode** `gun` (`src/player/modes/gun.js`): C / Ctrl crouch, RMB rise
  over (re-judged as the view turns), lean round tall cover; heard.
- **The clips** (`src/enemies/gunClips.js`): kneel; deathSlump,
  deathSlumpWall, deathSlumpOver, deathSlumpOverHi.
- **The sounds** (`src/audio/sounds/c2_guns.js`): the crack past the ear,
  the hit, the cock and the bolt (the tell), magazine and drum, wood hit and
  break, the doors, the desk's fall, the flanker's run.

## The second pass: what changed, as a player would feel it

**Played cold first** (the Browser pane at a quarter speed, then headless
plays): the cards came without their keys (the shim's text), the kit's low
line said "Get to cover" to a man already down behind the desk, the rise
card came before I was behind the desk, the rooms were mid-grey and the
frosted glass dark, the street a flat slab under a grey sky. Under the hood:
after the first beat the men's grid had every doorway shut (a shut leaf
shrunk to a point still blocked the doorway's middle), so men in later beats
could not come through doors at all - the old proofs passed against men who
could not reach me.

- **Lane C1's kit in, the shim gone**: the beats declare their objective
  lines; the cards are C1's with the key drawn (`[C] get low`, `[RMB] rise
  and aim`, `[R] reload`, `K.teach(id)`; the crouch once more in a beat
  failed twice; a beat played cold teaches what it needs); the fall is
  `game.fail` (the kit's words, `K.why()`); the ready beat's rise card only
  once I am behind the desk.
- **The warning reads the moment** (`K.warning()`, every step): `Stop him!`
  (the heavy close), `Your cover won't hold. Move!`, `They have your angle.
  Move!` (down, a man has me - whatever my health), `One's going round on
  your left/right.` (a flanker on his way), `Get down!` (hurt, standing in a
  line), `Stay down. Let it mend.` (hurt, down, nobody on me). Held a second,
  never flickering. And a line that changes its words is one warning: the
  kit's "a second before every fall" is no longer given again by each
  change of words (`[C2]` in C1's `ui/kit.js`, one line: a bad player stood
  at 1 hp through a street of such changes).
- **Men through doors as men do**: the grid has every door as it stands
  open (the leaf a thing to go round), the dead out of it, locked doors
  shut; a man whose way crosses a shut door reaches for it, it swings, its
  sound, through (a leaf that would swing into him: a step back first); a
  locked one rattles and holds; an entry that unlocks a door opens the grid
  through it; a way that will not open in 12 s is given up (`guns_doors`).
- **Standing up in the open is the losing way, cover the winning one**:
  the fire tokens are room for the player who fights from cover - a man
  standing up draws every gun; a man standing still in a gunman's line is an
  easier mark (his settle x0.6); a gunman whose mark stands up, with nothing
  coming near him, keeps firing (two more bursts) instead of going back. The
  pistol's round stays the brief's 17 (tried 24 and 20: still in the open
  against two, down in 2.6 s - under the brief's three to four - and the
  good and clumsy hands suffered).
- **In time to turn**: a man who has me from off my screen (a flank, a door
  at my back) needs 0.45 s more before his settle, and the warning says
  `Behind you!`. Nobody waits the clock out: nine seconds without a line on
  me, a man comes straight in (a pin once stood still for 150 s).
- **The Tommy gunner pins, he does not delete**: wider, climbing faster (a
  flank by him is not one burst and down). The heavy: 250 hp (five revolver
  rounds), a hit stops him 0.45 s, shorter bursts further apart. The pin's
  line says the moment: `Down through the burst. Up when he reloads.`
- **The lean** works where it should (round a jamb or anything over a
  standing eye, on the view's own line); under ~1.4 m is risen over.
- **Heard**: the crouch down and up, the rise over the cover.
- **The look**: the rooms darker (pools are the light), the corridor lit so
  its doorways frame the men as shapes, a pool inside room A's door (a man
  coming through steps into the light), a pool on the lobby's reception
  counter, the frosted glass lit from behind
  by the nearest lamp on the far side (the men are shadows on it), the
  warehouse lower with the city's towers over its parapet and a lamp over
  each opening (pools and rain in their cones).
- **The proofs widened**: `guns_clumsy` (WAVE_C §7's first-timer, three
  tries a contest), `guns_doors`, `guns_walk` (the jank walk and the lean);
  every try's record now says how fast a fall came and where the rounds
  into me came from (pose, kind, flank, through cover); the good hand shoots
  shadows on frosted glass, moves on the warning and off a spent spot,
  watches a man going round, ducks when hit.

## Shared files changed (each marked `// [C2]`)

- `src/player/Health.js`: `damage(amount, from)` - a hit's splash at the
  edge it came from; the mending later and slower while `exposed()`.
- `src/player/Weapon.js`, `src/player/weapons/revolver.js`: in a kit's
  encounter the round is the kit's (`G.guns.playerRound`); the revolver does
  not force its 1.2 s stagger on a kit's gunman.
- `src/enemies/Hood.js` (`die`): a death clip of the kit's is taken.
- `src/game/Game.js`: `killHood` lets a brain pick the fall
  (`brain.deathKind`) and emits `hood:down`; `this.bodies = new Bodies(this)`
  and its per-step `update()` (a no-op with no bodies). (The merge kept
  C1's `Stills` import beside it.)
- `src/ui/kit.js` (lane C1's, second pass, one line): `warnAt` is kept while
  a warning stays up and only its words change, so `Game.spareLast`'s "a
  second before every fall" is not given again by each change of words.

## Left (most felt first)

1. **Not yet played by a person.** The Browser pane cannot hold the
   pointer lock and stops drawing while hidden, so the second owner played
   the ready and door beats by hand only at a quarter speed with input sent
   from the page, and the rest by the plays and frames. The rise's speed,
   the lean, the tell's read at a distance and in the dark, the crack past
   the ear, the new warnings' words and timing: unjudged by a hand on the
   mouse.
2. **`guns_bad` is at the edge.** Standing and trading with a fair hand
   loses 2 - 5 of the 6 contests run to run (the final run 4; the run
   before on the same code 2). The door beat is won by holding the doorway
   (its lesson), the street often by the Tommy gun. Levers tried: the
   pistol's round 24 and 20 (bad lost 4 - 5, but standing still in the open
   went down in 2.6 s, under the brief's three to four, and the good and
   clumsy hands suffered), the fire tokens for a standing man, his settle x0.6,
   two more bursts at a standing mark. Next levers if a person finds
   standing too easy: `maxFiring`/`dmgK` per chapter (the difficulty table),
   or the street's men already out when it opens.
3. **The proofs are noisy**: one try per contest for idle and bad, three
   for clumsy; between runs a contest flips. The good hand passed twice in a
   row on the final code (and failed single contests in earlier runs on
   other code: two_ways, pin, street - flanks while down).
4. **The good hand lands 69 - 77 % of its rounds** (the revolver's beats 68 -
   82 %; the brief: 60 - 70 %). A wider hand was not tried again this pass.
5. **The street's men are there when it opens** (`cover: true`), not
   walking in; only the fourth walks in.
6. **A door's leaf does not stop against a body** (it stops against the
   living and the player): a door shut on a man who fell in its doorway
   would pass through him. The test bed never shuts a door then.
7. **Door handling is proven on the killhouse's six doors** (push and pull,
   locked and not); a door that swings both ways (`spec.both`) is untested
   with the men's reaching for it.
8. **Looks**: the frosted glass glows evenly and bright (pane 1's lettering
   is washed out); the pendant shades read as glowing discs from below; the
   near car's lens is a white oval in the street (the block's sedan, as in
   VIII); the warehouse wall is long and plain. The kneel, the Tommy
   gunner's exposed drum change and the heavy's walk: frames and strips
   only, not seen in motion by eye.
9. **`game.bodies`** is wired and probed but nothing in the test bed steers
   with it (the gunmen use the Hood brain's path following and the kit's
   `resolveMan`); C4 is its first user.
