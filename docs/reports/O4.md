Branch `ow/O4-combat` (from `c26e306`), port 5374. Brief:
`docs/briefs/O4_COMBAT.md`. Built and proven on the base's block (the avenue
under the el, the diner) until the city (lane O1) merges. This report is the
handover: a later agent continues the lane knowing only the code, the brief
and this page.

Play it: `?start=o4` (from the first contest), `?start=o4.<beat>`, a frame:
`?scene=o4&beat=<beat>&t=<s>&hud=1`. The beats of the test bed
(`src/scenes/94_o4.js`, not in the story's order):

contest won, walked off or ended by a gun drawn goes on to the next (brawl1,

hospital (the station after the police or a cop). The first beat of a save
gives the night's kit once: $150 and the revolver with 18 rounds in the coat
(`KIT`, `nightKit` in `94_o4.js`); from then on the money and the guns are what
was won, bought, kept or taken.

## What there is, as a player feels it

### The crowd brawl (`src/combat/brawl/`)
One fist fight for one to four men (`Crowd.js`, `crowdOf(game)`, the men's
numbers `kinds.js`, the edge ink `ink.js`; the footwork and look are the
controller mode `crowd`, `src/player/modes/crowd.js`).
- **The one I face is the one I look at.** The mouse turns the view freely;
  when it rests a fifth of a second the view eases onto the man I face (his
  chest): a soft lock within a cone of a radian. The man nearest the middle
  of my view (0.85 rad, a little sticky) is the one I face; looking at
  another switches to him and the ease follows him there over 0.2 s. His
  name and his bar are at the top (the fight's own bar, `FightCards.health`).
- **The ring.** The others stand round me at 2.5 - 3 m, each at his own
  angle, kept apart, circling slowly; one steps in at a time (0.75 s at
  least between two), two at most at once, and never two big blows on me
  together (1.5 s between two cued blows). A man out of my sight bides his
  time (his clock at half) and throws only the big ones, which come with the
  cue; as he steps in from out of my sight his feet and a grunt are heard
  from where he is and the ink closes in on that side of the frame.
- **My verbs (the rulebook's).** LMB / RMB: that fist's jab on the press
  (contact 0.06 s later); still held at 0.25 s the same fist cocks (the
  heavy: the c4 wind clip, its ticks) and goes when let go, or by itself at
  0.6 s. SPACE held: the block. A / D: a hop aside (0.55 m in 0.1 s), S a hop
  back; held on, circling and backing. E: take a bottle or a chair in reach
  (the place lists them; its point is its middle, within 1.5 m of where I
  stand and 0.8 rad either side of where I look; while its prompt is up the
  soft lock lets my eye rest on it); a press swings it once and it breaks on
  him. The wheel: a gun I own comes out and the fist fight is over (below).
  Nothing locks me over a quarter second: a blow that finds me holds my
  hands at most 0.25 s, and A / D cut that short after 0.04 s (a hop out of
  a stagger), a fist or the guard after 0.1 s; a jab jammed on a man's
  shoulder 0.25 s; a hop goes at once off his guard and 0.03 s into my own
  blow.
- **His guard and his openings.** Facing me, his guard is up: a jab into it
  is caught (a knock, my fist back) as often as his kind's `guard` says, and
  answered as often as its `answer` says (a fast straight, three white
  strokes at his fist, no cue: SPACE blocks it). A heavy into his guard
  breaks a lesser man's (the drunk's, the driver's, the hood's; the cop's on
  the second; never the boxer's). He is **open** when a blow of his missed
  (slipped, stepped out of, a straight blocked) - he stands where he is, his
  hands down, for its `miss` time x his kind's `open` (the `recover`
  state), and my first blow then is a counter (x1.5); when he breathes (a
  hand lowered a moment, every few seconds by kind); stunned after my
  heavy (knocked back a metre, into a wall or a car if one is there: more
  damage); for the first four of my blows that find him (the combo; then he
  covers up and steps back); with his back to me (x1.25). A jab into his
  wind-up is caught on his shoulder and his blow lands through it ("Caught
  swinging."); a heavy into it stops the blow and lands as a counter.
- **The dodge cue (his big blows).** Hooks, body hooks, haymakers, the shove:
  at its commit the world drops to a third, the side it comes from goes
  black and the safe side white (`FightCards.cue`, the base's ink). A / D
  toward the light within the window slips it clean (he misses: open); in the
  0.12 s after it, a glancing blow (half, no stagger); the wrong way or
  nothing, it lands. The window is 0.45 s of real time at first, 0.03 s less
  after each clean dodge, never under 0.3 (G1's hand-tuned rule). The safe
  side is the hop that really leaves the blow's path (tested both ways
  against the path and the walls), so a man behind me has a right answer
  too. The very first cue of a save holds time with the kit's card `[A] [D]
  dodge toward the light`.
- **The men** (`kinds.js` `MEN`; costumes `src/cast/kinds/o4_*.js`): the
  drunk (a pale raincoat, a bowler pushed back: slow wide haymakers, a gate
  for a guard, 30), the driver (the trucker's jacket and cap: 36), the hood
  (a fedora, a long dark coat: fast, feints, gets up once: 40), the cop (the
  tunic and the peaked cap with its badge: blocks well, shoves - the grab:
  48, gets up once), the boxer (bare-armed, taped fists: the best, 80; the
  gym's three levels in `BOXER_LEVELS`). Each tells before each blow (the
  glint, the grunt, his body; the small ones the white strokes); each goes
  down and stays down, or gets up once (`o4_getUp`, a clip of its own) with a
  quarter of himself and slower.
- **The end.** All down: won. My health at none: down - never unwarned
  (`One more and you're down.` up a second first, the last blow spared till
  then), the black with the fix in six words (`Too slow. Dodge when it
  slows.`, `Wrong way. Toward the light.`, `Into his guard. Wait for
  openings.`, `Caught swinging. Hit when open.`, `Block his jab.`, `Don't
  block the big ones.`, `From behind. Keep them in front.`), or `Hands up.`
  if a cop is standing. In the open (no bounds) I can walk off: once free
  of them (none within reach) my run is counted the way my feet go; a run of
  10 m `He's coming.` (they follow), of 30 m they give up (`He's gone.`, the
  fight `left`) and walk off (gone at 26 m or 10 s); one of them back on me
  and the run starts again.
- **A gun in a fist fight** (`crowd.draw(gun)`, the mouse wheel while a
  fight is on, any gun I own): the fight is over (`drawn`) - they run
  (`He ran.`), the gun is in my hand in the gun's footwork, and the city's
  answer is fed: `G.emit('crime', { kind: 'draw', cop })`. A round fired
  after a runner (the `shot` event) puts him down where he ran (the blood,
  `crime` `shot`); rounds never pass through him. `cfg.draw: false` keeps
  the wheel out of a fight (nothing uses it); the gym's bout drawn on is
  off and the bet is his.
- **The cards** (the kit's, once a save, ids shared with the base's fight):
  one at a time, 5.5 s at least from one's start to the next, and only in a
  safe second (no man in his blow or stepping in, no cue). `[LMB] [RMB] hit
  when his guard drops` first, as the first man walks in (0.8 s into the fight
  seen); the dodge at the first cue (held: time crawls, my feet stop with it,
  and it goes the moment I answer the cue); `[SPACE] hold to block his jab`
  after a jab finds me; `[LMB] keep it pressed: the heavy` at the first
  stunned or missed man once I have hit an opening. The nudge: eight punches
  into guards with none into an opening brings the opening card once more;
  three cues not answered bring the dodge card once more. A cue takes any
  other card down at once, and the queue with it. The fight waits for the
  place to be seen (the fade lifted, the place's card going): nobody steps
  in under it, nobody steps in before 2 s, and no cued blow comes before 5 s.
- **Hits read three ways.** Mine: his head snaps (or he is knocked back and
  staggers), a hit stop (jab 4 steps, 67 ms; heavy and things 5, 83 ms), the
  frame's kick, the inked burst at the jaw, the sound. His: my hands (the
  hurt clip from his side), the eye shaken, the ink pressed in on his side
  of the frame and lifting (no blood on the lens: a fist is no bullet), the
  sound.

### The guns in open ground (`src/combat/guns/cover.js` and the gun kit)
- **Cover from the world**: `worldCoverPoints(game, nav, area)` reads every
  collider in the area - tagged or not: a wall's faces and its ends, a post,
  a crate, a car - into the places a man can hold (a man's width off each
  face, the long ones every 1.5 m and their corners). The gun kit takes them
  when an encounter begins `{ world: true }` (`Covers.rebuild`, an `[O4]`
  line). `coverFor(game, at, from)` answers "where near here is a man hidden
  from there" (low or tall) for any system. For me nothing new was needed:
  the gun mode's C / RMB already gets low behind anything and rises over it.
- **The city's gunmen** (Hood variants, `[O4]` in `Hood.js`): `cap: 'police'`
  (the peaked cap, the tunic to the hip, the badge) for the police,
  `cap: 'flat'` (a flat cap, a short coat) for the second gang; the fedora and
  the long coat are the first gang's (and the detectives'). Any kind
  (`pistol`, `tommy`, `rusher`) wears any of them: `K.spawn({ kind, variant:
  { cap: 'police', ... } })`.
- **The street** (`street`): four of the gang down the avenue, the kit's
  rules (the tell, the settle, the fire tokens, the flank, the warnings,
  `K.why()` on the black), maxFiring 2, flankAfter 14, settleK 1.2. I start
  down behind a sedan across the near lane whose body is in the rounds' and
  the eyes' world (its glass seen through, its steel not): hands off, nobody
  has a line on me for the first seconds. It is mine, not theirs: its
  collider is `noCover` (their plans do not hold its far face 2 m from me).
  It takes rounds like any car: smoke at 40 %, black smoke and a flame at
  75 % (`The car's burning. Move!`), then the blast (`Smoking car. Get away
  from it.` if it puts me down); each try stands a whole one there again.
  After each fall in the contest (three at most) the flank comes 3 s later
  and their rounds are 7 % lighter (the fist fight's mercy, for guns).
- **The black's line** (`K.why()`, `[O4]` in `Guns.js`): the pose that took
  most of the last 6 s's damage (`mostHurt`) picks the fix, not the last
  round's pose alone; a car's blast within 1.5 s names the car.
- **The shotgun** (`src/player/weapons/shotgun.js`, the prop
  `src/props/kinds/o4.js`, the hands `src/player/clips/o4.js`, its sounds
  `src/audio/sounds/o4.js`): a side-by-side double. A press a barrel, nine
  pellets in a cone that reads on the wall; a pellet's damage full to 6 m,
  falling to nothing at 26 (a man down with one barrel at 4 m, nearly at 10,
  untouched at 25: the range play); through the gun kit's cover in an
  encounter; the pellets that find a man are one blow (one flinch, a burst
  each; half of him in one and he is thrown back a step). RMB to the
  shoulder (the cone a third tighter, the lens 6 degrees in). R breaks it:
  the barrels drop, the hulls fly out over the shoulder, the left hand
  brings two from the coat and thumbs them in, it snaps shut (1.45 s for
  two, 1.15 for one; a press shuts on what is in and fires). The count:
  the breech's two shell heads and the coat's shells in rows of five,
  bottom right.
- **Health** mends out of fire (the base's rule, unchanged).

## For other lanes
- **A place starts a bout / any system spawns a fight:**
  `crowdOf(game).start({ men: [{ kind, at: { x, z }, yaw?, c?, name?, hp?, over? }], bounds?, leave?, to?, maxIn?, dmgK?, things?, onEnd(result), onDown(info) -> { why, to } })`.
  `kind` one of `drunk | driver | gangster | cop | boxer`; `c` a Character
  already standing there (the gym's boxer, a hijacked driver: lane O2), else
  it is spawned (`MEN[kind].cast`); `over` numbers over the kind's (a level);
  `bounds` a rectangle the fight is held in (a ring, a bar; none: the open
  street, and I can walk off); `things` `[{ kind: 'bottle' | 'chair' |
  'stool', object, take() -> Object3D, available() }]` (E takes one);
  `onEnd('won' | 'left' | 'drawn')`, `draw: false` (keep the wheel out); my fall is the kit's fail: `onDown` gives its words
  and beat (default: the black's fix, or `Hands up.` with a cop standing; lane
  O3's arrest and the hospital go here). Events: `crowd:start`, `crowd:tell`,
  `crowd:cue`, `crowd:hit`, `crowd:down`, `crowd:up`, `crowd:end`,
  `crowd:shot` (a runner put down), and the city's seam `crime` `{ kind:
  'draw' | 'shot', by: 'player', at, cop }` for lane O3's heat. A thing's
  `object` is offered by its bounding box's middle (no grip point needed).
  `crowd.stop()`, `crowd.clearMen()` (the men off the stage), `crowd.active`,
  `crowd.target`, `crowd.stats`. A cop at heat 1 (lane O3): `kind: 'cop'`.
- **How cover is asked of the world:** an encounter `gunsOf(G).begin({
  world: true, area })` gives its men the world's cover; `coverFor(G, at,
  from)` for a system of its own (the police, the gangs). When O1's city
  merges its colliders are read as they are: nothing to tag (a tag only
  keeps a thing out: `SKIP` in `cover.js`, or `collider.noCover = true` on
  one thing - the player's own car).
- **The men of the city with guns:** `K.spawn({ kind: 'pistol' | 'tommy' |
  'rusher', at, way, variant: { cap: 'police' | 'flat', coat, brim, tall } })`
  (a gang's man, a cop, a detective). From the pavement's graph when O1
  merges: give `at` and `way.steps` from it (the seam).
- **The shotgun:** `weapons.equip('shotgun')`; `W.setCounts({ rounds,
  pocket })`; `save.ammo.shotgun` through `arms(G).to('shotgun')`.
- **Money:** `src/combat/money.js` (lane O7's HUD reads `save.money` and the
  `money` event `{ money, delta, why }`).
- **In the city, a gun out wants the gun mode** (`controller.setMode('gun')`:
  C gets low, RMB rises over cover); the test bed does it on its gun beats.
