Branch `ow/O3-people`, port 5373. Brief: `docs/briefs/O3_PEOPLE.md`. Built on

## 1. What exists (as a player meets it)

`?start=city` (BEGIN): the city of O1, now with people in it.

- **The crowd.** Up to 18 walkers round me (downtown 16, the Tenements 14,
  the Docks 9, the Hill 6, the Yards 4, the river road 4), on O1's pavement
  graph: walking (a random walk along the kerbs and over the crossings,
  keeping right of the walk line), stopping a while at the tram's stops, the
  stalls, the benches, the call boxes (facing the street), looking round,
  walking on. They come on 34 - 70 m off, **out of my sight** (behind me,
  round a corner) and go when out of it; none ever appears in view. A third
  under open umbrellas (carried up in the right fist; dropped when they run),
  a drunk now and then in the Tenements and the Docks (slow, weaving across
  the pavement), a couple under one umbrella now and then downtown and on the
  Hill (she at his side, on his way), the newsboy hurrying.
- **No two alike** (OPEN_WORLD's *nothing copy-paste*): 21 looks
  (`src/people/costumes.js` + four kind files): eight men in fedoras (coat
  lengths, coat greys from black to a pale raincoat, brims, scarves, two
  builds), four women in cloches (fur collars, scarves, coat greys), the
  base's bowler man, woman, old man with his paper, docker, dock hand and
  trucker, a newsboy; picked by a seeded hash of where a man comes on, from
  his district's table (the Docks and the Yards in work clothes, the Hill in
  good coats), a look already within 30 m passed over; each man a little
  taller or shorter (x0.955 - 1.045) and a pace of his own (x0.88 - 1.12).
- **They react**, seen: I walk into one - he is shouldered aside, flinches,
  looks at me ('Hey!'); a car coming - the dash clear (2.6 m off its line, the
  wall side checked), unless it gives him under a third of a second (or under
  0.6 s at over 18 m/s): then it **hits** him - down, dead over 9 m/s, thrown
  a little along its way, the car jolted; the gun out (RMB) near them - they
  run, with a cry, the umbrella dropped; a shot - they scatter; a fist fight
  - the near ones back off and cower, the rest stop and watch in a ring; a
  body or a wreck - after a while they gather 4 - 7 m off and watch.
- **The witness.** A crime a civilian sees (within 25 m, in line of sight)
  sends one of them (two for a shot or a death) to tell it: he **looks at me**
  (0.9 s), then **jogs** (3 m/s: seen going, catchable) by the pavements to
  the nearest call box (O1's 56) - or to a cop in sight, if nearer - lifts the
  receiver (its sound), and the heat comes when he is done. **Stopped**: put
  down (a punch, a round), or caught - I stand at his side and he gives it
  up, cowering. A crime nobody sees raises nothing.
- **The beat cops** (four looks: three of the copy's peaked caps and long
  coats with the badge, and the tunic of `o3_patrolman`), 1 - 3 round me by
  district (downtown 3, the Hill 2), walking a beat, stopping at a corner,
  having a word with a man of the street (the two face each other a while);
  at HEAT 0 one lets me by, his head turning as I pass. A cop who sees a
  crime blows his whistle: it is his.
- **The heat** (`police.heat` 0 - 5): **five police shields top right** (the
  stand-in of O7's badges: lit paper white for the level, the rest dark),
  breathing while they search; the line `HEAT n` as it rises, `Lose them.`,
  `Clear.` as it goes. A crime seen raises it to its level (a punch 1, a
  hijack 1, a shot 2, a cop knocked down 2, a death 3, a cop shot or run down
  4); more of the same while they hunt me pushes it a level higher.
- **The answer**, by level, each seen coming: **1** the nearest beat cop (or
  one sent from out of sight) walks over - `[SHIFT] run [LMB] fight` once, as
  he comes - `Hands up.` at 3 m, and the cuffs 1.3 s later unless I run
  (Shift: he sprints after me, ten seconds of wind, then runs) or punch him
  (the fist fight: the base's Brawl, his name COP on the bar; won, he lies
  there and his whistle brings HEAT 2; lost, the arrest); **2** a patrol car
  with two men, its **siren** heard a street away before it is seen, the
  **red lamp** on its roof: it drives the roads to me, stops by me and its men
  get out and run me down, or - me in my car - pulls up across my nose, its
  men at my door: boxed and stopped 4 s (`Hands up.` at 1 s) is the arrest;
  **3** two cars, and **a roadblock** on the road I am driving, 75 - 140 m
  ahead while they see me: two cars nose to nose across it, their lamps, a
  man behind each; **4** the detectives' car: two men out of it with a Tommy
  gun and a pistol (the base's gun kit: the raise, the glint, the settle,
  cover, flanks) and every uniform on foot draws; **5** everything, and the
  riot squad's lorry with three men.
- **Losing them.** Out of their sight (a cop on foot sees 36 m, a car 55 m,
  42 m while it prowls) for 20 s at 1, 32 at 2, 60 at 3, 70 at 4, 80 at 5:
  first **the search** (2.5 s unseen: the sirens drop to a yelp now and then,
  the cars prowl the streets round where I was last seen, the men on foot run
  to the place and look round it, the shields breathe), then `Clear.`. Found
  in the search: the heat as it was. A report (a box, not a cop who saw me)
  starts them at the place, and the clock waits until they are there (30 s
  at most). A door shut behind me unseen (`police.lost()`, O5) or a change of
  car unseen (`police.changedCar()`, O2) loses it at once.
- **The arrest** is the fail (`game.fail({ why: 'Arrested.', to: 'station' })`):
  the black, and I wake on the pavement outside the police headquarters
  (O1's landmark; O5's room when it merges) facing the street: the heat gone,
  the gun gone (RMB: `no gun`), the car gone (a coupe at the kerb down the
  street until O2 parks the city's), the money less $50 (`save.money`), the
  words `Released. Fined $50. They kept the gun.` Back in control 2.9 s after
  the black. **Down** (shot) is the hospital (`to: 'hospital'`, the gun kit's
  words for the fall), beaten by a gang man `Beaten.` - the stand-in's place
  is the pavement at Baxter St and Fifth St (no lot of the plan is a
  hospital yet: O4/O5).
- **The gun back**: E over a man down who carried one (a cop, a gang man)
  takes his: `E  take the gun`.
- **A police car** standing empty (its men out) is mine with E (the city's
  own `get in`): a hijack the men see.
- **The gangs.** The Docks' crew (long dark coats, fedoras, white scarves:
  three looks + `o3_docksman`) and the Tenements' boys (flat caps, short
  jackets, a grey V of shirt: three looks + `o3_tough`), in threes of three
  different men on their corners (the bars' corners first, the rest 110 m
  apart), spawned out of my sight within 130 m: they watch me (heads turn
  within 20 m, they face me within 6 m); stand with them and one says a word
  (`Something you want?` / `Lost, mister?`); punch one or shoulder one at a
  run - he squares up (the fist fight); draw near them (22 m, in their line)
  - they draw (their own figures to the gun kit: no change of look), shoot
  one - his three draw; about one in ten minutes, near me on their ground, a
  Docks three and a Tenements three meet at the border: fists (7 s), then
  guns (the street's own fight: flashes, shots, men down), a crime of
  theirs - the street scatters, a patrol car drives to the place and its men
  stand over it, no heat on me unless I join in.

## 4. For other lanes

  `game.crime.emit(kind, point, by = 'player', { victim, level })` - kinds
  and levels in `crime.js` (`punch` 1, `hijack` 1, `smash` 1, `shot` 2,
  `assault` 2, `death` 3, `cophurt` 4; `menace`, `wreck`, `body` 0: the street

  emit its own too). A crime nobody sees is nothing; the record says who saw

  `save.money` is read and written by the fine.

- **O2 (wheels)**: replace `makeCar(police, { x, z, yaw, kind })` in
