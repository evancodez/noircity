# O5 — places (lane report, the handover)

Branch `ow/O5-places`, port 5375. Brief: `docs/briefs/O5_PLACES.md`. Built on
lane O1's city (`docs/seams/WORLD.md`). This report is the handover: what a
player meets, how it is built, how to add a room, the seams other lanes call,
what was proved and what is left. (Section 6 holds the proofs' numbers.)

## 2. How it is built

| file | what |
| --- | --- |

| `src/places/rooms/<kind>.js` | **the rooms** (gym, diner, bar, gunshop, garage, hotel, police, theatre, hall, hideout, stair): `{ kind, spawn, build(K, site, places), live(K, site, places) -> { step, enter, leave, dispose }, wake(K, site, places) }` |

| `src/city/kits/interior/` | **the interior kit**: `frame.js` (a place's frame), `kit.js` (RoomKit: rooms, walls with holes, floors, stairs, rails, lamps, doors, seats, uses, props, figures; merged by material and part), `door.js` (the killhouse's door made the kit's: push either way, locked + rattle, glazed, double, stops on a man), `pieces.js` (counter, back bar, stool, chair, table, desk, bench, bed, shelves, crates, heavy bag, ring, safe, lockers, bars, lit window, sheet, labels on one atlas), `shell.js` (the lot's face again with its holes), `surgery.js` (a place's own pieces taken out of the city's merged meshes while it is loaded, put back when dropped), `mats.js` (the interiors' materials with their ink surface classes) |

**How a room stands in the city.** A place's lot is a mass of O1's far build
(one box among a tile's merged thousands). While the place is loaded: the
lot's base box is taken out of its tile's mesh (its 36 vertices moved onto one
point; the bullets' copy too) and drawn again as a shell with holes at its
doors and the room's windows (same material, same ink id: from the street the
building is as it was); the dark panes of O1's near build at those doors and
windows are taken out the same way; the lot's collider box is switched off and
a wall round its edge put in with gaps at its doors; the room is built behind
the face. Dropped, everything is put back as it was and the room's geometry is
freed. Nothing of this happens while no place is loaded, so the city's frames
are untouched (the golden check, §6).

**Light.** A room's lamps join O1's lamp deal (`lamps.addExtra`): inside, they
are the nearest, so they take the real lights (two with shadows) and the
cones; a room is the reference's few hard pools in the black. Lit panels (a
back bar's glass, the kitchen, a jukebox's tubes, the screen) are the ink's
`lit` surface class.

**Floors.** Floors stack (the hotel, the hideouts' upstairs, the stairwells,
the hall's gallery): the walking surface picks, of the floors under me, the
one nearest the floor I am on (a flight of stairs within a step of it wins:
I go up); each floor's walls, doors and uses are that floor's (`lvl`), so a
man on another floor of the same plan walks through them (the collider's
`gone`, set each step). Figures are kept off upper floors (one surface query
serves everyone: it reads the player's floor).

## 3. For other lanes

- **Enter / wake**: `placesOf(game, stage).enter(id, { at: { x, z, yaw }, seat })`
  puts me inside a place; **`.wake('police')`** (O3: after an arrest; the
  city's beat `station` does it: `game.fail({ why, to: 'station' })` wakes
  me on the cell's bench); the city's beat `hotel` (CONTINUE after a sleep).
  `game.city.places` is the instance in the free roam.
- **Events**: `places:enter` / `places:leave` / `places:load` / `places:drop`
  `{ id }`; **`crime` `{ kind: 'brawl' | 'glass' | 'theft' | 'hideout', pos,
  level, place }`** - the stand-in for O3's `crime.emit` (O3: listen, or
  replace `Places.crime`); `money { n, total, why }`; `matchbook { id, n }`;
  `bout { won, level }`; `shop:buy { id, price, item }`; `heat:clear` and
  `night:hour` (the hotel's sleep: O3 clears the heat, O7 moves the night);
  `saved { at: 'hotel' }`; `hideout:cleared { id }`; `theatre:reel`;
  `bounty:look`.
- **O3 (police)**: `police.lost()` is called (if it exists) after 20 s inside a
  place; `police.clear('sleep')` at the hotel's sleep (if it exists). The
  station's cells are `K.cells` of police HQ.
- **O4 (combat)**: the stand-ins to replace: `src/places/bout.js` (the bout:
  `brawl.bout(level, bet)` when it lands - the gym's `live` returns its run),
  the bars' fight (`rooms/bar.js` `barLife`: two men, `stepIn` - O4's crowd
  brawl and its spawn), the hideouts' men (`rooms/hideout.js`: `K.men` lists
  each man `{ kind, x, z }` in the room's frame; `hideoutLife` spawns them with
  the gun kit's `K.spawn({ kind, at, start })`, `start` held until they turn on
  me; the revolver given by `arm()` - O4's carried guns replace it), the gun

- **O2 (wheels)**: the garage (`rooms/garage.js` `carWork`: `car.repair()` if
  the car has one, else `damage` / `pull` zeroed; repaint: the car's paint

- **O6 (smash)**: shop windows are the base's Glass (`stage.glass.addPane`;
  broken: its collider goes, `crime 'glass'`); a room's furniture is merged by
  material (a chair that goes over wants its own mesh: draw it from the room's
  record, as the kit's `prop()` does for the base's props).
- **O7 (the night)**: the bounty board's place `K.bounty` (police HQ), the
  matchbooks (`save.matchbooks`), money (`save.money`), the hotel's save (the

- **O1**: `lamps.addExtra(list)` / `removeExtra(list)`; a place's own street
  doors are skipped by `city.js`'s door code (`places.owns(door)`), O1's
  `city:door` still gets `e.handled = true` for them.

**Adding a room.** A file `src/places/rooms/<kind>.js` default-exporting
`{ kind, spawn, build(K, s, P), live? }`, a site in `sites.js` with that kind.
In `build`, author in the place's frame (door at the origin, +z the street,
the room at z < 0, `s.lb` the lot): `K.room(x0, x1, z0, -0.3, { h, sides: { s:
{ holes: P.doorHoles(s, 's') } } })`, furniture from `pieces.js`, lamps, seats,
uses (`K.use({ at: [x, y, z], prompt, onUse })`), figures (`K.fig(kind, x, z,
yaw, { state, seat })`). For street windows: `K.windows = P.faceWindows(s, ...)`
and their holes `P.windowHoles(K.windows)`. For an upper floor: `K.at(n)`
before building it.

## 5. Where the brief was changed in play

- **The bout's stand-in** is lost at its downs (four at the first level,
  three at the second, two at the third): the base's fist fight gets me up for
  ever, and a bout must be losable to mean anything. The first two men are the
  base's manners slowed (`bout.js` `STYLE`).
- **The hideouts' men** hold until they have had me in sight 2.2 s (or I fire,
  or hit one): the warning first (`They know your face.`) and my revolver out
  with it, so a player who walks in sees the tell and may fire first or back
  out through the door. They do not follow me into the street (O3 / O4's).
- **A street door has a closer** (it swings shut 3.5 s after I am clear of it)
  and **is pushed open** when I walk into it: a push door is pushed.
- **Loaded near, drawn when seen**: "loaded on entry" would build a room in the
  frame E is pressed (a hitch at the door); a place is built as I come within
  ~34 m, and not drawn while its door is shut and I am outside it.
- **The theatre's lobby is the Orpheum's** (O1's `theatre1`); the Bijou stays a
  rattling door. **The pier office** is the shipping office on the quay by Pier
  7 (a Docks lot: O1's shed on the pier is one merged iron box with no door).
- **The diner's and the theatre's doors** are a shop front made the entrance
  (their lots have no door in O1's layout); that front's dark pane is taken out
  while the place is loaded.
