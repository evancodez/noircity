## 1. What exists (as a player meets it)

`?start=city` as O1 left it, and now the city has its wheels:

- **The fleet**: eight kinds, each its outline at 40 m (`src/vehicles/kinds.js`):
  the **coupe** (the start's is the base's full model; the traffic's a light
  build of the same shapes), the **saloon** (the tall greenhouse, the spare on
  its back; the gangs'), the **taxi** (a checkered band along its belt, a lit roof
  lamp), the **police car** (white front doors, the red lamp on its roof - the
  registry's crew red, lit only while it hunts - and the siren on its wing), the
  **hearse** (the long black coach, landau bars), the **delivery van** (a short
  bonnet, a tall box), the **lorry** (a cab, a stake bed, its load under a tarp,
  dual rear wheels), the **tram** (on Grand Ave's rails). Each has its spec on
  Car.js's bicycle model (the lorry slow and a wall, the police car fast), its
  seat (a cockpit with the wheel, the dials, the mirror, the wipers put into
  whichever car I sit in: `src/vehicles/cockpit.js`), its lamps, its note.
- **The traffic** (`src/traffic/`): cars on O1's road graph keeping their lane,
  stopping at the **signals** (hung over every signalled junction on their span
  wires: a head facing each street, three lamps in it - top lit STOP, middle the
  change, bottom GO - white, the ink's; a late amber run), giving way at a
  junction to a car already in it (and to me crossing on my red), braking for
  the car ahead and going round a slow one (the base's Pilot), stopping for a man
  in the road (a horn, then a slow way round), sounding the horn when I cut them
  or knock them, pulling over for my horn (Q), and **panicking** at a shot or a
  man down (`wheels.alarm(point)`: they swerve, stop crooked, the man at the wheel
  ducks). They come on out of my sight (100-200 m off, round a corner or in the
  fog) and go out of it; three in four come toward where I am. Fourteen at most,
  the four nearest ahead with their lamps' cones and pools, the rest their lit
  lenses; their engines (the two nearest) by their speed.
- **The parked cars**: at the plan's bays, a share of each district's of its
  kinds (lorries and vans in the Yards, saloons on the Docks, the hearse on the
  Hill), the same every night; drawn as instances of their kind (near, light and
  box builds: a few draws for all of them); solid to cars (they shunt, rock and
  settle) and to men.
- **The tram**: up the east track and down the west of Grand Ave under the el,
  over the crossover at each end (a double-ended car: it stops and runs back),
  its stops, its bell when something is on its track (it stops for it), the red
  signals; lit windows, its lamp, its rumble. Not drivable: a moving wall.
- **The hijack** (`src/vehicles/hijack.js`): E at any car's door -
  - a car I left: `E  get in`;
  - a **parked car**: `E  break in` - 2 s of the lock at the glass (its sound),
    the controls mine through it (walk off or turn away and it is left); a
    witness in sight (a man at the wheel of the traffic near) sees it - the crime
    event says so (O3 reads it);
  - a **car with a man at the wheel** (he brakes when I stand at his door):
    `E  take it` - I am in his seat (eased), he is shoved out of his door (0.8 s)
    and answers: a **civilian** runs; a **man with a jaw** comes back for his car
    (`He’s coming back.`) - drive off, or he has me out and we fight on the
    pavement (the base's Brawl, one man; lost: he gets back in and drives off; won:
    the car is mine); the gang's men step back from their doors and **draw** (the
    raise first; the base's Hoods' rounds) - drive; a **cop** fights and calls it in
    (crime 'cop'). What was in it is mine: a wallet now and then ($).
  - a car whose man ran from it in a panic: `E  get in`.
  Leaving: E as O1 had it; the car stays where I left it (kept in the city as a
  record when I am far, made again when I come back: six at most).
- **My car's knocks**: the scrape, the crash and the dent heard, sparks off the
  tin, the jolt in the view; its **damage** shown (the base's steps: a lamp out,
  the bonnet steaming, the wheel pulling, `She won’t take much more.`, then a
  wreck: dead, black smoke - I get out and take another). Q the horn.
- **The garage** (the Yards, its street door): in a car stopped at the door
  `E  into the garage` (kept, up to three; mended for $10 a step of damage if I
  have it); on foot at the door `E  try the door` opens its card (my cars there:
  W / S choose, A / D the grey - black, dark grey, pale grey: $10 - E brings it
  out to the kerb under a short black).
- **The fares**: in a taxi, a man or a woman on the pavement on my way hails (an
  arm up); stop by them (E, or two seconds stood there) and they get in; the
  objective line says where (`The Park.`, O1's starts as places); the meter runs
  by the distance; stop there and they pay. A hard knock: `Easy.`; a second: `He
  got out.` (the fare lost); left sitting 20 s: `Come on.`, 20 s more and he gets
  out.
- **The race**: at the Yards' corner (the Straight at Water St), three cars on
  the line with their lamps on and a man with a flag; stop by them and E
  (`E  race them  $10`, or for nothing with nothing): a short black - me on the
  pole - the flag up, `[W] go when the flag drops`, three laps of the Yards' loop
  (up the Straight, Centre St, down Foundry St, Water St: 680 m a lap, all right
  turns, kept clear of parked cars and traffic while it runs); the line says
  `Lap 2 of 3. Second.`; `They’re ahead.` on the last lap if I am behind; first
  over the line is the purse ($30, or $5 for glory).
- **Money**: `save.money` (a getter on the save's flags until O4's lands): the
  wallets, the fares, the race; spent at the garage and on the race's bet.
