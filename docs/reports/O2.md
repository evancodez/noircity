# O2 — wheels (lane report, the handover)

Branch `ow/O2-wheels`, port 5372. Brief: `docs/briefs/O2_WHEELS.md` (with the
[must] added on 2026-10-23: a variety of cars, nothing copy-paste). Built on
O1's city (main at 23d0f73; main merged in again at df912f9: the title's
rename, the standard's new musts). Lanes O4 (combat) and O6 (smash) were not in
main while this was built: their seams are mine for now, one function each,
named in §4.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## 1. What exists (as a player meets it)

`?start=city` as O1 left it, and the city now has its wheels.

- **The fleet** (`src/vehicles/kinds.js`): eight kinds, each its outline at 40 m,
  each in two or three variants a player sees, each car's own look by a seeded
  hash of where it stands (the user's must: nothing copy-paste):
  - the **coupe** - a fastback, a roadster (its top down), a sidemount (the
    start's coupe is the base's full model; the city's are this light build);
  - the **saloon** (the gangs') - the fastback with its spare, the two-door
    notchback with its trunk, the sidemount with a rack;
  - the **taxi** - the checkered band, two-tone, the checks on its doors; its
    lit roof lamp;
  - the **police car** - white doors, black and white, the spotlight; the red
    lamp (lit only while it hunts) and the siren;
  - the **hearse** - landau bars, or the curtained coach;
  - the **delivery van** - the box, the panel with its painted name (LAUNDRY,
    BAKERY, BREWERY, ICE & COAL), the pickup with its load;
  - the **lorry** (slow, a wall) - stakes and a tarp, the box, the flatbed;
  - the **tram** on Grand Ave's rails (not drivable).
  On top of the variant: three greys (black, dark, pale), whitewalls or not,
  and on a parked one now and then a scrape along a door or a flat tyre it sags
  on (`lookAt(kind, x, z, salt)`). Each kind has its spec on Car.js's bicycle
  model (the lorry slow and heavy, the police car fast), its seat, its lamps,
  its engine's note. Any of them can be sat in: the cockpit (the dash, the
  dials, the wheel, the mirror, the wipers: `src/vehicles/cockpit.js`) goes into
  whichever car I take, at that kind's seat; its body opens a well for it and
  its own glass goes (the windscreen's rain is the cabin's).
- **The traffic** (`src/traffic/`): cars on O1's road graph that keep their
  lane, stop at the **signals** (hung over every signalled junction on span
  wires: a head facing each street that comes in, three lamps - top lit STOP,
  middle the change, bottom GO - white, the ink's), run a late amber, give way
  at a junction to a car already in it and to me crossing on my red, brake for
  the car ahead and go round a slow one (the base's Pilot), stop for a man in
  the road (a horn, then a slow way round), sound the horn when I cut them or
  knock them, pull over a moment for my horn (Q), and **panic** at a shot, a man
  down, or `wheels.alarm(point)`: they swerve, stop crooked, the man at the wheel
  ducks. They come on out of my sight (100-200 m off, round a corner or in the
  fog), three in four toward where I am, and go out of it; fourteen at most; the
  four nearest ahead carry their lamps' cones and pools, the rest their lit
  lenses; the two nearest are heard by their speed.
- **The parked cars**: at the plan's bays, a share of each district's in its
  kinds (lorries and vans in the Yards, saloons on the Docks, the hearse on the
  Hill), the same every night; drawn as instances by kind and variant (near,
  light and box builds), each instance its own grey; solid to cars (they shunt,
  rock, settle and stop against their neighbours), to men, and to rounds (their
  box builds near the eye).
- **The tram**: up the east track and down the west of Grand Ave under the el,
  over the crossover at each end (a double-ended car: it stops and runs back),
  its stops, the red signals, the bell for anything on its track (it stops for
  it); its lit windows, its lamp, its rumble. A moving wall.
- **The hijack** (`src/vehicles/hijack.js`), E at any car's door:
  - a car I left, one whose man ran from it in a panic, O3's police car stood
    with its men out, the phone box's: `E  get in` (O7's any-car E in `city.js`,
    through `getIn`: it is my car now, the one before it kept where I left it);
  - a **parked car**: `E  break in` - 2 s of the lock at the glass (its sound,
    `…  the lock` low in the frame), the controls mine through it (walk off or
    turn away and it is left); the crime is O3's (`game.crime.emit('hijack')`:
    its walkers and cops judge who saw it);
  - a **car with a man at the wheel** (he brakes when I stand at his door):
    `E  take it` - I am in his seat (eased, 0.35 s), he is shoved out of his door
    (0.8 s) as **the street's own man** (O3's Person, held out of its brains
    while he is mine: a car that hits him knocks him down) and answers: a
    **civilian** runs to the call box (or a cop in sight) and tells it (O3's
    witness: the heat comes); a **man with a jaw** turns back for his car
    (`He’s coming back.`) - drive off, or he has me out (a car all but stood)
    and we fight on **O4's crowd brawl** (down: the black, `He wanted his car
    back.`, the hospital, and he drove off in it; won: `The car’s yours.`, he
    lies there and gets up as the street's men do); the gang's men step back
    and **draw** (`They’re drawing.`, the raise first) and, if my car stands,
    walk up to its side window and fire through the glass - drive, or be shot
    down at the wheel (`Shot sitting at the wheel. Drive.`, the hospital); a
    **cop** calls it in at once (heat 2: O3's patrol cars, the siren) and comes
    back for me on **O3's street fists** (down: the arrest). A wallet now and
    then in the glovebox ($).
  Leaving: E as O1 had it; the car stays where I left it (six kept; far ones as
  records, made again when I come back).
- **The view** (V in a car, the pad's R3: `src/vehicles/chase.js`): the seat, or
  a camera behind the car - a spring on its heading and place (it lags into a
  turn and under the brake), over the roof, in along the line on a wall or a
  column (never through one), the look swings it round the car and it settles
  behind, reverse looks back the way the car goes, RMB held is the seat; eased
  both ways; remembered in the save (`save.flags.carView`); the car whole from
  behind (the cockpit, the hands and the screen's rain go; the man's bust at
  the wheel shows). Taught once on a long drive (`[V] the view`). The man is
  still in the seat for the city (`G.eye` there: who sees him, who shoots him).
- **My car's knocks**: the scrape, the crash and the dent heard, sparks off the
  tin, the jolt in the view; its **damage** is O6's rule (`src/destruct/
  CarDamage.js`: dents where hit, the lamp out, steam, smoke, fire, the wreck;
  my `damage.js` stands aside wherever O6's set is on the stage). Q the horn.
  The player's car never turns on the spot by itself: held into a thing with no
  steer it stays against it, and S backs it off (`Car.unstick`, [O2]).
- **The garage** (the Yards): in a car stopped at its door `E  into the
  garage` (kept, up to three, in the save's `save.garage`; mended for $10 a step
  of damage if I have it); inside (O5's room) the mechanic, when my car is not at
  the kerb: `my car, kept` - my card (W / S choose, A / D the grey - $10 - E
  brings it out to the kerb under a short black). O5's repair and repaint of the
  car outside are his.
- **The fares**: in a taxi a man or a woman on the pavement on my way hails (an
  arm up; the street's own man while he waits: a car that mounts the kerb at him
  knocks him down and the fare is gone); stop by them (E, or two seconds stood
  there) and they get in; the line says where (O1's starts as places: `The
  Park.`) and **the drop-off is marked**: a lantern lit at its kerb (a pool of
  light on the pavement from the city's lamp deal), the ring on the minimap and
  the map (O7's `game.things`); `Here.` within 60 m; stopped within 30 m he pays
  (the meter by the distance). Driven away from it for 15 s: `Wrong way.`; his
  patience is the road's length at a cab's pace and half a minute (`Come on.` at
  three quarters, then `He got out.`); sat still (not at a red, not behind a
  car) 20 s `Come on.`, 40 s out - the clock never runs back. A hard knock:
  `Easy.`; a second: `He got out.`
- **The race**: at the Yards' corner (the Straight at Water St) three cars waiting
  one behind another at the kerb by the line with their lamps on, and a man with
  a flag up the kerb ahead (on foot: `E  the race` - `Bring a car. Ten to
  race.`); stop by them in a car and E (`E  race them  $10`, or for nothing with
  nothing): a short black - the three of them on the grid and **me at the back**
  (they show the way on the first lap) - the flag up, `[W] go when the flag
  drops`, **two laps** of the Yards' loop (up the Straight, Centre St, down
  Foundry St, Water St: 680 m a lap, all right turns). Each corner is **marked
  on its outer kerb** (a white board on a trestle, black chevrons pointing right,
  its lantern lit: `src/vehicles/marks.js`), the line says `Two laps. Right at
  every lamp.` then `Lap 2 of 2. Second.`, the next corner is the minimap's ring;
  off the course the warning points the way back (`Back on the course: to your
  left.` / `... behind you.`), out after 9 s off it; `They’re ahead.` on the
  last lap behind; first over the line wins the purse ($30, or $5 for glory) -
  a dead heat is theirs. The field drives at its own 0.84 / 0.80 / 0.76 of the
  course's speeds every race (no easing after a loss). After the flag the
  others run out past the line and pull in to the kerb; the corner is set again
  when I am away and it is out of sight.
- **The police car**: O3's patrol car is the fleet's police car (`Patrol.js
  makeCar`, [O2]: its variants, its man at the wheel, solid to rounds; the riot
  squad's a fleet lorry), its pilot sees the parked cars, and a unit wedged for
  good (three times running) is replaced out of my sight. My own hunt
  (`wheels.police(x, z, { target, siren })`, a `HuntPilot`) is still there for
  the beds (`&hunt=1`).
- **Money**: O4's wallet (`src/combat/money.js`: `save.money`, O7's HUD counts it
  in): the wallets, the fares and the race through O7's `game.things.end`;
  spent at the garage and the race.

## 4. For other lanes

- **A car at a point** (O3's patrol, a phone box's delivery, a gang's car):
  `game.wheels.carAt(x, z, kind, { yaw, driver, paint, lights, look })` -> a Car
  (snapped to the nearest lane and its heading when `yaw` is null). Give it a
  driver: `new Pilot(game.wheels.lanePathFor(from, to), opts)` (the base's Pilot
  on a lane path), or the hunt below. Step it yourself, or put it in
  `wheels.hunters` (stepped with the traffic).
- **The police car** (O3): `Patrol.js makeCar` now spawns the fleet's police
  car (`wheels.carAt(x, z, 'police')`, its lamps owned, the man at the wheel
  shown; a riot squad's 'truck' is the fleet's lorry) and O3's red dome on its
  roof as before; `PatrolCar`'s pilot reads `wheels.bodiesFor(v)` (the parked
  cars too); `Police.replaceCar(pc)` sends another when one is wedged three
  times running out of sight. O3's driving (driveTo, driveDirect, the box, the
  roadblock) is unchanged. My `wheels.police(x, z, { target, siren })` (a
  `HuntPilot`, `src/vehicles/police.js`) is kept for the beds (`&hunt=1`).
- **`wheels.alarm(point, k)`** (O3's police, O4's guns): the traffic within
  38 m * k panics. The player's `shot` and a `hood:down` already call it.
- **The hijack's events**: game `'wheels:hijack'` `{ car, how: 'parked' |
  'pulled', driver: 'civilian' | 'fighter' | 'gangster' | 'cop' | null, seen }`;
  the crime is O3's: `game.crime.emit('hijack', point, 'player', { level })`
  (level 2 for a cop's car, and the cop raises the heat himself: `police.raise(2)`).
  `'wheels:fare'`, `'wheels:race'`, `'wheels:garage'`, `'wheels:view'` { third }.
  The fare and the race are O7's things (`game.things.start('fare' | 'race', {
  line, at })`, `.line`, `.end` with the money).
- **O3**: the men a hijack pulls out, the fare who waits and the race's flag man
  are your Persons (`people.spawn(look, x, z, yaw, 'o2')`, `mode 'fight'` while I
  hold them; handed back with `role 'walker'` / `'cop'`); the pulled civilian is
  sent to report with `people.startReport(p, rec)`.
- **O4**: `answers.js` uses your crowd brawl (`crowdOf(G).start({ men: [{ kind:
  'driver', c }], leave: true, onDown, onEnd })`, the man's own Character) and
  your `money.js`. The gang's gunmen are still the base's Hoods (`Game.hoodFire`;
  at the glass a close shot of their own, since O6's bullets meet a car as its
  hull's box) - the gun kit (`kitUse`) would be the next step.
- **O6**: `damage.js` stands aside where `stage.smash.cars` is (your rule for
  every car of `game.vehicles`). The race's corner boards are registered with
  your set as 'fence' (a car knocks them over).
- **O5**: the garage room's mechanic opens my card (`wheels.garage.open()`) when
  my car is not outside and the save keeps one (`save.garage`).
- **O7**: the view's key could go on the title's keys line (`V view`) - not done
  here, to keep your golden title frame; the card `[V] the view` teaches it in a
  drive. `G.eyeFix` puts `G.eye` in the seat while the view is behind the car.
