# O6 — smash: the destruction set, car damage and fire (lane report, the handover)

Branch `ow/O6-smash` (from main at 23d0f73: O1's city merged), port 5376.
Brief: `docs/briefs/O6_SMASH.md`. **The seams for every other lane:
`docs/seams/SMASH.md`** (the registry's API, the kinds and their numbers,
the cars' rule, the caps, how O1 / O2 / O4 call it). This report says what
exists as a player meets it, how it is built, how it was proved and what it

## 1. What exists (as a player meets it)

**In the city** (`?start=city`, O1's free roam, every district): the
generator's furniture is the set's. The ~665 lamp posts, 100 hydrants, 117
bins, 56 call boxes, 106 benches, the market's 10 stalls, 108 street-name
posts, 18 tram-stop signs and 77 telegraph poles stand as O1 drew them
(merged in their chunks: the city's frames are unchanged) and answer:
- **A car** (any car of `game.vehicles`): a lamp post leans from its base,
  bends and stops (a hard blow, past 40 km/h: down flat across the
  pavement), its light flickers a second and dies - its pool, its cone,
  its real light and its glowing mouth in the far city go dark; a hydrant
  is sheared off its stem, its head flies, a white column of water stands
  over the stump for a minute (rings on the wet round it, a thing to drive
  through); a bin is knocked flying, lands, rolls; a call box, a bench (a
  newsstand: the bed's; the city places none) go over and lie where they
  fell; a stall goes over, its canvas
  torn off fluttering, its goods spilled and skidding; a street sign or a
  telegraph pole bends over. Below its speed each is a solid thing (a
  post stops the car). The car pays: speed (a lamp post half of it, a
  hydrant, a call box or a stall a third), the eye kicked, damage (two
  lamps and it steams), the sound of the thing. **Every break is seen**:
  white where it went (the muzzle flash's splatter for two frames, sparks
  off iron, white splinters off wood and stone, a pale puff), a hydrant's
  water white, papers, a stall's canvas and its goods pale enough to read
  lying in the dark.
- **What lies is physical**: a stall, a newsstand, a call box, a bench
  over on the pavement stops a man (a box where it lies) and slows a car
  (a bump through the wheels); a bin, a crate, a hydrant's head, a fence
  panel lying in the road is kicked on by the next car; a post down across
  the road is a bump; planks, fruit, papers, canvas and glass lie where
  they fell and are kicked by a car's wheels. Nothing floats, nothing is
  inside a wall.
- **A round**: a lamp's head goes out (glass, sparks, the pop, often a buzz
  back on, then dark); a hydrant spits a fan of water from the hole; a bin
  is knocked over; a stall's goods jump and burst (the sixth takes it
  over); a newsstand throws papers; a bench splinters; a sign is bent over
  at the third or fourth; a call box loses its glass, then sparks; **a
  shop window** is glazed where the round went and holes, stars and goes
  (a car scraping the fronts bursts them too).
- **A man on foot**: walking into a standing bin, call box, newsstand,
  bench or stall is a clang; at a run a bin goes over. By a car that burns
  he is told `She’s going up. Get back.`, in a wreck's fire `Too hot. Get
  back.`, on the tram's rails `The tram. Off the rails.` (met, knocked
  aside off them and hurt); downed, the fail says what did it.
- **It mends out of sight**: what is more than 200 m from the eye is put
  back standing; past 140 things out at once the oldest at rest that the
  eye does not see goes back. A chunk built again keeps what is down down.

**Cars** (the rule for every car, mine or theirs): dents where it was hit,
the wing crumpled at a hard corner, the headlamp on that side out, a door
hanging and swinging after a side hit; at a hard blow (any speed) lane
C3b's smash: the corner down on its broken spring, the body twisted onto
it, the glass on that side and the screen gone (a sedan's), grains of glass
on the road, the bonnet sprung. The engine by its damage: steam (1.5), grey
smoke and the power going (2.5), **fire** (3.3: flames at the bonnet's
seams, black smoke, the fire light; in it: `She's burning. Get out.`), then
7 s later (3 s if past 4) **the blast** (the white flash on the wet, the
shockwave ring, the shards, what stands within 7 m goes over, cars near
hurt, a man near hurt, a man in it: the fail `She went up with you in
her.`) and **the wreck**: Wreck.js's burnt shell cut to the car, on its
rims, twisted onto its worst corner, burning down to embers, solid, never
driven again. A round near a wheel flattens the tyre (the corner down, the
grip gone, a front one pulls the wheel; the rim sparks on the road). One
blow from below the fire never wrecks a car (the warning first).

**The bed** (`?start=smash`, not a scene of the game): the city's own Grand
Ave between Centre St and Fifth St, under the el - every kind placed along
both kerbs among the city's own, the west side's shop fronts glazed with
awnings over them, a coupe at the kerb (E in), a sedan (its door locked:
E rattles it) and a lorry parked across the way, the tram on its rails (a
bell; it shoves a car in its way and strikes sparks off it - a man in it
may get out as it shoves; a man on its rails is warned, then knocked aside
and hurt), the Tommy gun in my hands (LMB, R; back in my
hands as I get out of a car). Beats: `bed`, `burn` (the sedan burning),
`tram` (the sedan across the rails), `hundred` (a hundred things in the
road round a burning sedan: the budget's moment; `&all=<t>` pushes every
one over at once), `fire` (my coupe burning at the kerb: the lane's one
loss). Look development: `&pose=x,z,yaw,pitch`, `&stage=<kind>@<t>[,<speed>,<dx>,<dz>]`,
`&vm=0`.

## 4. For other lanes (the short of SMASH.md)

- **O1 (the city)**: the generator's records are registered by `props.js`;
  a new kind of street furniture is a row in `smashKinds.js` and a line in
  `KIND_OF`; a merged mesh must record its ranges (`stream.js` shows how).
- **O2 (wheels)**: every car you spawn gets the damage rule by being in
  `game.vehicles.list`; give your models `model.meshes.paint` (and `lens`,
  `chrome`, ...) as children of `model.group` at the identity and
  `spec.top`; a sedan's `v.car` keeps its glass. Your tram: `kind = 'tram'`
  and call `stage.smash.collide(car)` from its kinematic driver (the bed's
  `spawnTram` is the example). Your lorry tears awnings by its `spec.top`.
  Parked cars: wrecks stay (frozen, solid); give the city's free roam a car
  to take when mine is a wreck.
- **O3 (people)**: `game.on('smash', e)` (what, where, by whom) for a
  witness; `car:burning`, `car:wreck`.
- **O4 (combat)**: rounds already reach the set (React.js); a fist, a
  thrown body: `stage.smash.hit(item, point, impulse, 'fist' | 'body')`;
  the blast of a grenade or the like: `stage.smash.blast(at, r)`.
- **O5 (places)**: an interior's breakables can be registered as objects
  (`register(mesh, kind)`); a place's shop glass as a Glass pane registered
  as kind `glass`.
- **O7 (the night)**: the bed is a test bed (not in the order); the fail
  `She went up with you in her.` goes to the current beat (the hospital is
  yours).

## 6. Where the brief was changed in play

- **A post gives, then holds.** Below its speed (a lamp 4.5 m/s, a hydrant
  3, a call box 3.5, a stall 1.8) a thing of the set is a solid that stops
  the car (Car.js's own give on the posts; `car.vsBox` for the boxes): a
  nudge at a crawl does not knock a lamp over.
- **What a car pays** (after the critic: it was too small - the coupe went
  42 -> 46 -> 61 km/h through two lamps, a sign and a hydrant). A lamp post
  or a telegraph pole costs about half the speed, a hydrant or a call box a
  third, a stall a third, and kicks the eye; two lamps at 50 km/h and the
  car steams, a kerb run of iron leaves it smoking, never burning (the
  first build's 0.1 a m/s over 3 set it on fire after four lamps; now 0.07
  for a lamp). Hard knocks still smash a corner.
- **A stall goes over whole** (it lies as an obstacle that stops a man and
  slows a car) with its canvas and goods flying, rather than coming apart
  into planks (a crate does that).
- **The call box goes dark** has no light to go: the city's call box has
  none; its glass breaks at the first round instead.
- **The city's awnings, crates, fences and shop glass**: O1's city places
  no crates or fence panels, and its awnings are the near build's relief:
  the bed has them all; in the city the awnings are left (§7). The city's
  shop windows are glazed when a round or a car meets one (§8, M8).
- **The fails**: in a car when it goes (`She went up with you in her.`),
  on foot by it (`Too close when she went.`), in its wreck's fire (`Burnt.
  Keep off the fire.`), on the tram's rails (`The tram. Mind the rails.`);
  each warned before it (in a burning car at once; on foot within 7.5 m of
  one; in a fire's reach; 26 m ahead of the tram), and the fuse is 7 s (3 s
  past damage 4).

## 7. Left (most felt first; rewritten after the critic)

1. **The city's awnings and the el's hung lamps are not breakable.** The
   shop glass is (§8, M8); the near build's awnings are relief merged with
   the facade, and the el's hung lamps are `stage.lamps` without a post of
   the set. The way: register the awnings from `nearLot`'s `awning` boxes
   as the shop windows are (a record, its range in the chunk's `tree`
   merge); give the hung lamps a `lamp` item with no post (`how: 'shot'`).
2. **What lies in the dark stays dark.** The break is white now (the
   splatter, the sparks, the splinters, the pulp, the papers), but a stall
   or a bench lying in an unlit stretch afterwards is a silhouette, by the
   ink's rule; only what carries light of its own (papers, canvas, goods)
   reads there. Paling the stalls' own ink would change O1's frames: not
   done.
3. **A shot-out lamp's head prints as a grey disc**, not black: its mouth's
   colour is 0 in every vertex (proved), its cone, light and pool are out,
   but the ink prints the empty disc a paper grey. Not a glow; unexplained.
4. **The tram wrecks a car it meets** in half a second (4.5 damage, the
   fuse 3 s): warned (`She’s burning. Get out.`) and E now gets me out of
   a car it shoves, but it is harsh; O2's tram may want to brake for a car
   standing on its rails.
5. **The gun's press-to-round latency** (O4's): unchanged (the critic
   measured one frame from the press to the thing's answer).
6. **Down in the city**: the set's own fail sends him back to the roam's
   start with its words (`why()`); the hospital is O3 / O7's
   (`smash.cars.why()` gives them the words).
7. **The first-draw warm** (§8, M5) draws ~40 meshes of nothing in every
   pass for the stage's first 20 steps. A lane that makes a material of its
   own lazily inside a blast (as `fx/Explosion.js` and `Glass.js` do) must
   add a twin to `CarDamage.prewarm`, or its first blast hitches again.
8. Dents are vertices pushed in (no normals redone); a sedan's are lost
   when world/Cars swaps it to its far build and back.
9. The lorry's seat in the bed has no wheel or hands (O2's lorry has its
   own cabin).
10. Fists reach the set only through `smash.hit(..., 'fist')`: O4's brawl
    does not call it yet (a man walking into things does now).
11. The coupe has no glass (its windscreen is the rain screen effect).
12. The `hundred` beat's errors dump does not finish inside the watchdog;
    it runs clean in play.
