## 1. What a player meets

- **Nobody is the same man twice.** Every man and woman of the street is
  varied by where he stands (a seeded hash of the place: the same corner,
  the same man; the next corner another), on top of the 22 looks of the
  street (O3's 21 + the swell): his **build** (slight, middling, heavy: the
  shoulders' width and the chest's depth), his **hat's set** (pulled down
  over the eyes, pushed back, cocked), his **carriage** (a slouch, the chest
  up, the head down against the rain), his **walk** (a woman's hips swaying
  and her arms in, a working man rolling his shoulders, a gang man's
  swagger, all with the gait's own phase), the **idle he keeps** (the
  weight on one leg and then the other with a look round, hands in the
  coat's pockets with the shoulders up, a cigarette to the lips - its ember
  the one warm pin in the black - or the arms folded) and **what he
  carries** (the cigarette; the swell's cane). Some walk with their hands in
  their pockets. The cops stand straight; the women and the working men are
  kinds of their own in how they move, not only in what they wear.
- **The swell**: a new kind, told at 20 m by the topper alone - the tall
  silk hat, the long black coat, the white scarf at the throat, a cane in
  his hand (downtown, more on the Hill).
- **Never stiff.** A man who must turn on the spot steps round (a slow step
  or two on the turn, no spin on his heels); a walker leans into a turn,
  harder the faster; heads turn to me as I pass within a few metres before
  them; a man who takes fright jolts the step it happens, then runs.
- **What a man means, before he does it.** A witness who sees a man knocked
  down points his arm at me, then goes to tell it (0.9 s later); a cop sent
  to me holds his palm out with his 'Hands up.'; a gang's three each put a
  hand into the coat, and the guns come out of it a third to half a second
  later (they were in the hand at once before).
- **The physics layer: every fall in the city is a body.** A man punched
  down, shot, or struck by a car falls the way the blow went, settles on
  what is there - the street, a kerb, a stoop's steps, against a wall, in a
  doorway - and lies still (no jitter, no sinking, never through a wall);
  the fall still reads as the base's falls (the arms thrown up, the knees
  going: the clip is what the body reaches for). A man knocked down gets up
  out of how he lies (the get-up blends from his pose: no pop). **A car**:
  slow, he is knocked down before it; fast (50 km/h), the bumper takes his
  legs, he goes onto the bonnet and is thrown on along its way (19 m at 50
  km/h, his highest point 1.6 m). A body lying in the road is rolled out of a
  car's way by it, not driven through. The blood pool spreads where he came
  to rest. A shot man against a wall goes down it; in a doorway he goes down
  in it.
- **The wanted man draws as himself.** The bounty board's man who draws on
  me is his own figure handed to the gun kit (no new gunman made where he
  stood: the figure no longer changes as he draws - O7's left).
- **The street answers a gun drawn by the wheel** (O7's draw on foot): the
  near ones run, the first the step it comes out (they did not before: only
  O3's stand-in RMB draw made them run). And a gun drawn is no longer echoed
  back as a punch by O7's stand-in crime bridge (the street cowered and
  watched a fight that was none: they run now, as they should).

**Where the brief was changed in play.** (1) No full ragdoll from the
start: the torso follows the fall's own clip in the world for the first
0.42 s, then physics - a man falls like the base's men (the "cool"
animations the user liked) and then settles on what is really there;
thrown (a car), he does not follow it at all. (2) Bodies are limp once
down: a body pulling its limbs to a pose against the ground pushed itself
along and hopped (found in the harness), so the limbs reach for the clip only
while he falls. (3) A car's face does not throw a man up faster than its
bonnet: his own legs dragged by a moving car lifted him 5 m (found in
`cartest`), so for 0.6 s after a car hit the upward speed is held to 2.4
m/s - he goes over the bonnet, not into the sky. (4) The two-bone IK for
feet on kerbs was not built: the city's kerbs are a 0.2 m ramp in the
walking surface (`groundAt`), so a man's root rises over 0.15 s as he steps
up - no pop to hide. (5) The "light figure" for the far ones is the Hood's
own third LOD (O3's choice, kept).

1. **The gestures are small at a distance in the dark.** The witness's
   pointing arm, the cop's palm and the hand into the coat are true (the
   numbers in `o8/intent`) but a dark arm against a dark facade at 10 m is a
   few pixels; the ink's rim (not this lane's) is what would carry them. A
   cold critic should name the intentions from strips taken under a lamp.
2. **A body face down gets up by rolling over** in the blend (0.55 s) into
   the get-up from the back (`o4_getUp`): a get-up from the front (hands
   under the shoulders, a knee) is not authored.
3. *(done after the critic: O5's `Places.spawnFig` styles them)* **The rooms' figures were not varied** (O5's `K.fig`: the barmen, the
   boxers, the drunks, the diner's): each is the costume as built. One line
   in `Places`' figure maker (`fig.setStyle(styleFor(kind, x, z, ...))`)
   gives them the street's variety - it moves `o5_bar` and `o5_gym`.
4. **The gun kit's own gunmen** (the hideouts', the police gunmen O4's
   `K.spawn` makes) keep the Hood's variants (coat, brim, height, a
   cigarette) and no style layer; they fall on the ragdoll like everyone.
5. **The stoop's fall is slow to settle** (3.5 - 4.0 s before the critic; on the
   final code round 1 of `o8/falls` settles at 4.98 s every run - over the play's
   own 4.5 s bar, so its round 1 FAILS on that alone - and round 2 at 3.98 s: the
   body slides down the steps a while; nothing under the surface, nothing into a wall); a body can also come to rest half across a step's
   edge with its middle lifted - the points are the joints, the mesh between
   them is not solid to a step's corner.
6. **No feet IK** (§2: the kerbs are ramps; a man standing half on a kerb
   has one foot in the air by up to 7 cm).
7. **The coat and the hat in the wind**: the coat's hem springs with the
   start, the stop and the turn (the Hood's); no wind of its own; a hat is
   not blown off.
8. **The cars' boxes are the coupe's and the sedan's shape for every car**
   (the lorry a 2.2 m wall); O2's new models will want their own two boxes
   (`carBoxes` in `ragdoll.js`).
9. **O3's `o3_crowd` and `o3_witness` lean on their bots**: the witness's
   chase failed once in two runs here (the bot), not the street.
