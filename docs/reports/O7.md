## 1. What the user should feel

The title is the city: Grand Ave under the el in the rain, the coupe at the
kerb, NOIR CITY and its red splatter over the dark storeys, BEGIN over the
wet street. BEGIN is that kerb; the title was the first frame of the night.
In play the screen is the street: nothing on it but what the moment needs -
a torn scrap of the inked map bottom left, heading up, me in the middle;
the police shields brushed in top right when they want me, half-toned
while they search, struck through as they give up, and a line top left
that is my situation (`Lose them.`, `Stay out of sight.`, `Clear.`, or the
thing I took up: `A car is coming.`, `Your car.`); the money counted in
when it comes and gone after; Tab for a moment's look at all of it, the
car's state and the hour. M is the map on the pause card with the world
held still under it; ESC the pause card with the map at its side, the
settings (the sound is there now), the matchbooks. F in a car turns a radio
dial (silent stations hiss until the user drops records in), the radio
fades with the door; the city hums and calls far off (the gasworks, the
river, the markets, a ship, the Hill's bell on the hour, a siren a long way
away). Into a car at no heat the night is kept, quietly (`saved`), and
CONTINUE puts me back in that seat. Things to find: the el's stairs (ride
the loop up front, off at any station; chased onto it while they search, I
am lost at the next station), the phone boxes (a car brought for $50), the
wanted board by the police headquarters (three faces a night). The revolver
is under my coat from the first second: the wheel (the pad's d-pad left /
right) draws it, LMB fires, and the city answers the shot within the second
(the shields, `Lose them.`, the paper's SHOTS FIRED); a lamp's head shot
out pops and goes dark (O6). The title's keys are the city's as built, one
line for the keys and one for the pad.
