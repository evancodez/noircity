# O1 — the city (lane report, the handover)

Branch `ow/O1-city`, port 5371. Brief: `docs/briefs/O1_CITY.md`. **The seams
for every other lane: `docs/seams/WORLD.md`** (the plan's format, the
generated city's fields, the graphs' queries with examples, the stage's
parts and their APIs, the kits, the budget, the free roam's events). This
report says what was built, how it was proved, what it measured, and what
is left. Review frames: `docs/review/O1/` (one a district). The city's golden
manifest: `shots/golden/city/` (`NOIR_GOLDEN=shots/golden/city python3
tools/golden.py check`).

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## 1. What exists (as a player meets it)

`?start=city` (BEGIN on the title; `&at=<id>` for `grand clock tenements docks
yards hill gasworks bridge river park`): on the pavement of Grand Ave under the
el, in the rain, by a coupe at the kerb. Nothing to do; the whole city to go to.

- **E at the car** (any door, looking at it, within a stride): in, eased into
  the seat (0.35 s: the camera never jumps); the card `[E] get in` once. The
  base's `Car.js` (W / S / A / D, SPACE the handbrake); the card `[W] drive
  [SPACE] handbrake` once. **E, stopped**: out by the driver's door (or the
  other, if that one is against a wall), its lamps off. **M** (the pad's d-pad
  down): the inked map, north up, my mark on it; M again closes it (in this
  scene M leaves the sound alone: `mapKey`); after 25 s of driving the card
  `[M] the map` once. **E at a street door**: `E  try the door` - it rattles,
  `locked` (every street door of every lot; a place's is O5's to open).
- **The city**, ~1.2 km square, five districts readable from a car:
  - **Downtown**: stone, towers with setbacks and spires, the el's loop over
    Grand Ave / Market St / the Bowery / Houston St with its arcs over the
    corner lots and three stations (platforms, kiosks, a stair down), the
    theatres' lit marquees and neon blades, the hotel's blade, the diner's
    tube, the police headquarters' globe lamps and board, the bank, the
    store, forty neon blades over shop fronts on the busy streets (blue, a
    green, an amber: no red), Broadway's diagonal with its flatiron prows
    aimed at **the clock tower on Clock Square** (four lit faces; the
    landmark down Broadway, seen over the roofs from the Hill and the Docks).
  - **The Tenements**: narrow lanes, two dead ends (Eldridge Ln, Doyers Ln),
    alleys through and into the blocks, fire escapes, water tanks on the
    roofs, laundry lines across the lanes, the market hall (barrel roof) and
    its stalls on Hester St, the gym, a bar, the gun shop, the station.
  - **The Docks**: warehouses with loading doors and painted names, the fish
    market's open shed, four gantry cranes on the quay, four piers (sheds, an
    office on Pier 7; their ends open: bollards), two slipways, a bar.
  - **The Yards**: the rail yard behind its fence (nine tracks, wagons, the
    signal gantry; in through its gate on Clock St), the gasworks (three
    holders in their guide frames, two stacks, its fence and two open gates),
    the long Straight (a 970 m run for the races), the garage, sheds with
    painted names.
  - **The Hill**: walled gardens with iron gates and a house each, trees over
    the walls, the park (railing, four gates, paths, lamps, the bandstand,
    trees).
  - **The river**: the river road along the east and south quays and round
    the bend, the quay's railing, the bridge out over the water on its arches
    to the far bank, shut at the toll barrier with a lorry across the road;
    the far bank a silhouette (sheds, lights, towers in the fog, the
    stadium's floodlights east, the fairground's wheel south-east).
- **Street furniture by rules**: ~670 lamps (goosenecks along every kerb,
  alternating on the streets and both sides on the wide ones; the quay's; the
  bridge's; the park's; hung under the el every other span), hydrants, bins,
  call boxes, **a street-name post at every junction** (the two names on
  enamel blades: where am I, read from the car), benches (downtown, the Hill),
  newsstands, the tram's stops and its rails down Grand Ave's middle,
  manholes, the market's stalls; parking bays along every kerb lane (data for
  O2: `C.bays`, `graph.baysNear`).
- **The river is the end**: on a slipway or near a pier's open end the warning
  `The river.`; in the water `The river took it.` (the car) or `Into the
  river.` (on foot) - the kit's black, and up on the quay at the nearest steps
  with a car at the kerb by me (the `ashore` beat), in about four seconds.
- **Collision everywhere a man or a car can reach**: the lots' footprints, the
  gardens' walls, the land's edges (the rail embankment west, the Hill's wall
  north), the quay's railing, the bridge's parapets and barrier, the fences,
  the el's columns and its stations' stairs, the posts (a lamp post gives to
  a car), the holders, stacks, cranes' legs, the fish market's columns, the
  stalls, benches, newsstands, trees; kerbs a car bumps up and a man steps;
  the cars as their boxes to a man on foot.

## 2. How it is built

| file | what |
| --- | --- |
| `src/world/plan.js` | **the plan** (data, one file): districts, streets as grid lines with their road segments, Broadway, the river road and the quay, the piers, slipways, the bridge, the el's loop and stations, the special grounds, the landmarks, the named places, the starts |
| `src/world/cityGen.js` | **the generator** (no THREE, deterministic, ~45 ms; `getCity()` caches it): cells -> kerb polygons (clipped by Broadway and the river road) -> lots by district kit (alleys, back masses so nothing is hollow, flatirons, towers, estates), walls, posts, lamps, furniture, bays, laundry lines, the specials; `groundAt`, `inWater` |
| `src/world/graph.js` | **the graphs**: roads (144 junctions, 113 with signals, 245 edges with their lanes; dead ends flagged) and pavements (663 corners, crossings, alleys); `nearestLane`, `route` (A*), `randomWalk`, `baysNear`, `pavementAround`, `pavementRoute`, `nearestPavement`, `lineOfSight`, `streetAt` |
| `src/world/map.js` | **the map** (`drawMap(C)`, `MapOverlay`) |
| `src/world/GridColliders.js` | the walking colliders on a 16 m grid (the same API as `Colliders`, plus the cars' boxes) |
| `src/city/kits/facade.js`, `street.js` | **the kits**: a lot's tiers, its one seeded window layout, its far build and its near build by district; the pavements, kerbs, the lamp post, the furniture, the street names' atlas |
| `src/stages/noircity/` | the stage's parts (WORLD.md §4) and `compile.js` (every program compiled and every buffer uploaded under the fade in) |
| `src/scenes/city.js` | the free roam |
| `tools/lib/o1_city.mjs`, `tools/plays/o1_*.json`, `tools/o1shots.sh` | the proofs' driver and plays; a list of frames in one go |
| shared, marked `[O1]` | `src/game/Game.js` (M is the map where the scene says `mapKey`), `src/util/RayWorld.js` (a stage with `rayAll` keeps its solids past 260 m: a round stops in a wall anywhere in the city), `tools/fpstable.mjs` (`MOMENTS=city`) |

**Streaming.** Two builds. The **far** build (always drawn, 240 m tiles, about
five meshes a tile: two ink groups of brick, two of stone, one of every light
by its vertex colour) is every lot's mass, its lit windows and shop fronts,
every lamp's mouth, the water tanks and spires: the silhouette and the lights
of the night city to the fog, never a gap, a bullets' solid everywhere. The
**near** build (96 m chunks within 180 m, built nearest first inside 4 ms a
step, dropped past 250 m with their geometries freed and their solids out of
the bullets' world) is the relief on those masses - dark panes, the lit panes'
bars, shop fronts' mullions, cornices, sills, doorsteps, fire escapes,
awnings - the pavements and kerbs, the lamp posts, the furniture, the street
names, the washing: six to eight meshes a chunk. Both read one seeded window
layout, so the relief lands round the lights already there: what comes in at
180 m is a few strokes in the fog, never a building. The landmarks, the el,
the river, the places and the neon are built once (they are few).
**The lamps' budget**: 30 cones and their rain / rim entries dealt to the
nearest lamps ahead every quarter second (a lamp keeps its cone while it has
one), five real SpotLights (two with shadows) to the very nearest; a cone close
to the eye dimmed (x 0.25 at 3 m, whole by 20 m) so driving the kerb lane
through them is not a white wall.

## 4. For other lanes (the short of WORLD.md)

- **O2 (wheels)**: the road graph's lanes, signals and bays; `stage.track`
  is the cars' world (`Car.js` as it was); `stage.cars` is the block's `Cars`,
  empty (`parked` is yours); `vehicles.spawn('sedan')` works here. The tram's
  rails are down Grand Ave's middle between the el's columns; the el's
  streets' outer lane is parking or slow. The free roam's car is
  `game.city.car` (`city:in` / `city:out`): your hijack takes over getting in.
- **O3 (people, police)**: the pavement graph (`pavementRoute`,
  `pavementAround`, crossings at junctions), `lineOfSight` for witnesses,
  `navAround(x, z, r)` + `new NavGrid(stage.colliders)` where a fight is (the
  city is too big for one nav grid), the station's lot (`PLACES` 'station',
  its door in `stage.part('places').doors`).
- **O4 (combat)**: cover is the city's own: the lots' corners, the el's
  columns, the holders, the stalls, the newsstands, the benches (all in the
  colliders, tagged); bullets stop in every wall (`rayAll`).
- **O5 (places)**: `city:door` (`e.handled = true` to open), every place's
  lot and door (`stage.part('places').doors`, `C.lots[].place`); the lot's
  far mass is there, an interior goes behind its street face.
- **O6 (smash)**: the furniture's records are `C.props` (kind, x, z, yaw,
  chunk) with their colliders in `C.posts` / `C.walls`; they are merged into
  their chunk's meshes - a thing that breaks wants its own mesh (draw it from
  its record, hide it from the chunk's merge by a flag on the record). The
  lamps: `stage.lamps.setOn(lamp, false)`, `lamp.bent`.
- **O7 (the night)**: the map (`MapOverlay`, `drawMap`) for the pause card;
  M is the map only where a scene says `mapKey`; the city's ten golden frames.

## 5. Left (most felt first)

1. **Hitches while driving are not proved gone.** The drive-through passed
   its hitch bar on a quiet machine (fcd6281: 3 frames over 50 ms in 5.3
   minutes, the worst 116 ms in the first seconds of the drive); the
   streaming test's loops (8d504c9) and the final run were made with three
   other lanes' headless Chromes holding the GPU at 96-100 %: 20 frames over
   50 ms on the final code, the worst (133-158 ms) in the first 23 s of the
   drive, as on the quiet run. Not isolated: the first seconds' spikes come
   after E and the first throttle (candidates: the car's sound bed made on
   the first entry into the car's room, the pooled shadow lights' first
   re-renders, the first chunks attached while moving); every program and
   every buffer, the chunks' own materials included, is now compiled and
   uploaded under the fade in (`compile.js`; the last change, made after the
   final drive), the near radius is 180 m, the sills four triangles, the
   quay's railing lighter (2.9 -> 2.1 M triangles at Grand Ave). Re-run
   `o1_drive.json` on a quiet machine; the recorder lists each slow frame
   with where the eye was and what the stream was doing.
2. **The city is empty**: no traffic, no parked cars, no people (O2, O3) - the
   streets read bare; the bays, the lanes, the signals and the pavements'
   graph are ready for them.
3. **Roofs and fire escapes cannot be climbed**, roofs are not walkable and
   not in the pavement graph; the el's stairs are solid but not climbable and
   the el cannot be ridden (O5 / O7's things to do).
4. **What breaks is merged into its chunk's meshes** (furniture, lamp posts):
   O6 must draw a breakable from its record on its own; a lamp post a car
   gives way to bends nothing you can see yet; a lamp's head cannot be shot
   out (`stage.lamps.hit` returns null).
5. **The el's train** goes round the loop once and is gone at the station it
   left (seen if you watch it stop): it should pull in and wait.
6. **The swim is a fade**: a man in the river is up on the quay after the
   black, no swimming.
7. **Variety**: the grid is regular (one diagonal, one bend, two dead ends);
   the Hill has no curving lanes; the Yards' sheds are plain boxes (no
   sawtooth roofs); the trees are lumps of ink; every street door rattles
   alike; the shop windows are dark panes with nothing behind them.
8. **The map** shows only me and my car (no heat, no places), is an overlay
   on M rather than the pause card's (O7), and two district names sit on a
   street's name.
9. **Stoops** are doorsteps only; the bridge's far end is a barrier by design
   (the far bank is a silhouette).
10. **The pavement graph's crossings** are a heuristic (pairs of corners
    across a street at a junction): no pedestrian has walked them yet (O3).
11. **Golden frames will move** when O2 / O3 put cars and people at the starts
    (expected: re-take `shots/golden/city`).

**Where the brief was changed in play**: the chunks are 96 m, not 64 (fewer
draws; the far build makes the size invisible); the near build is relief
only - the masses and their lights are always drawn (the "far chunks as black
masses with lit windows" of the brief, but for the whole city at once and
cheap: no building ever pops in); the river's man is put on the quay by the
kit's black rather than swimming (the fail's rule: a warning, the words, back
in seconds).
