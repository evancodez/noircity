## What exists

- **The plan** `src/world/plan.js`: a 1.2 km city as data (x east, z south):
  five districts by rect (the Hill north, the Yards west, Downtown in the
  middle, the Tenements east, the Docks south), 12 north-south and 15 east-west
  streets with their segments (dead ends in the Tenements: Eldridge Ln, Doyers
  Ln), Broadway's diagonal through five cells (flatirons), the river road
  along the east and south quays round the bend, the bridge to the shut far
  bank, the piers and the slipways, the el's loop and its three stations, the
  special grounds, the landmarks, the named places, the starts.
- **The generator** `src/world/cityGen.js`, **the graphs** `src/world/graph.js`,
  **the map** `src/world/map.js`, **the kits** `src/city/kits/facade.js`, `street.js`.
- **The stage** `src/stages/noircity/` and **the free roam** `src/scenes/city.js`.
